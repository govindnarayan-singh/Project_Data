The preamble is as follows:-

Several world-class manufacturing companies from countries in the vicinity of the COVID-19 outbreak are exploring opportunities for industrial investment in other countries in order to reduce dependence on one country.

In this context, the Working Group chaired by the Minister (Industry) on how to attract foreign investors to the State and take measures to boost the growth of industry in such a situation, has presented effective and innovative measures to facilitate the investment of world class multinationals and underground companies in the State of Maharashtra by the Department of Industry to the Cabinet of Ministers.
