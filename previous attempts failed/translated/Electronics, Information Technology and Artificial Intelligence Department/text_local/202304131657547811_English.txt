Government Clearing Paper -

The decision to approve the Indian Service Chartered Officers (IAS), Indian Service Police Officers (IPS), Indian Service Forest Officers (FS) in the service of the Government of Maharashtra from the Financial Provision of the Department of Information Technology for Supply of Laptop/iPad/Tablets for the Promotion of e-Governance as Required has been issued by Government Decree No. 3814/Prc.174/39, dated 24.02.2015.

Paragraph 1 of the Governing Decision dated 24.02.2015 shall read as follows.

"Charter officers of the Indian Service (AS), Police officers of the Indian Service (IPS) and Forest officers of the Indian Service (IFS) in the service of the Government of Maharashtra shall be authorized to purchase modern equipment under the applicable rules and regulations to promote e-Governance from the financial provisions of the Department of Information Technology for the purpose of supplying laptops/ipads/tablets.
