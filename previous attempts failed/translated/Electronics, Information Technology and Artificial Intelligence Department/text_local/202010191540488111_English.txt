Letter from O/S-1/39 dated 21st September 2020.

The preface is as follows:

The State Government has placed office attendance under control as a precautionary measure to prevent the spread of coronavirus (COVID-92) in state offices and to prevent the spread of infection to state officials/employees. It has been shown that it is affecting the functioning of the government to some extent. The Government has been relying on the Department of Work from Home in this regard.

Taking into account the above, the meeting was concluded on 10th September 2020 under the chairmanship of the Chief Minister under the guidance of the Minister for the Efficient Use of the E-Office System in Government Operations in order to accelerate the use of computers in Government Operations of the State Government, ensure orderly functioning of the Government Operations, secure documentation and facilitate the decision-making process by rapid and expeditious obtaining of information.

Government decision:

The implementation of the NIC's e-Office (URL) mahaeoffice.maharashtra.gov.in system in the Ministry is urgently required. All the officers/staff of the Ministry departments have been trained in the e-Office system before. However, if there is a need for further technical assistance/training in this regard, contact the nodal officers associated with the Department of Information Technology e-Office as well as the e-Office Support Team.
