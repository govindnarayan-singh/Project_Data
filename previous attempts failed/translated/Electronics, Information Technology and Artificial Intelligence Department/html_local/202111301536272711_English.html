Background

The Government of Maharashtra has a number of welfare schemes to

provide benefits, financial assistance and services to the citizens of the state.

Many of these schemes offer financial benefits to eligible applicants. The

government has developed the MahaDBT portal for direct disbursement of

benefits to the Aadhaar linked bank accounts of the beneficiaries and it has been

gone live in October, 2018.

The process for disbursement of Direct Benefit has been prescribed vide

Government Resolution under reference. It was noticed that there is a need to

bring changes in the process of disbursement of benefits to make it more easier.

The proposal to make changes was under consideration. Accordingly, the

following orders are being issued regarding the changes in the disbursement

process of MahaDBT Portal.

Government Resolution ;-

For the disbursement of benefits, financial assistance and services in the

state. The Direct Benefit Transfer Portal (Maha DBT) has gone live in October,

2018. Process has been prescribed vide Government Resolution under reference

for the disbursement of benefits through Portal. As disbursement process needs

some changes to make it more convenient, the decision was taken to revise the

disbursement process in the meeting of the High power Committee held on

September 27, 2021under the chairmanship of Hon. Chief Secretary. As per the

decision, Para.no. 8 and 9 of the Govt. resolution under reference is amended as

follows;-

Para. 8-. After the amount is received by Drawing and Disbursing

Officer (DDO) in their scheme specific Bank account from Treasury, the

DDO shall transfer the said amount to the specific department scheme

"Central Pool Account” at the earliest. For this purpose, department

should open a separate "Central Pool Account” with State Bank of India

for the respective scheme. This central pool account of the department will be used by respective department only for the use of benefit disbursement to beneficiary using MahaDBT portal and will be integrated with Public Finance Management System (PFMS). It is necessary that department to open this central pool account in State Bank of India as the SBI is integrated with MahaDBT portal and PFMS both.

Para 9. - Once the fund is credited to the department scheme "Central Pool Account", disbursement will be initiated, and the benefit shall be transferred to the respective beneficiary or authorized Institution/Agency's account through Public Financial Management System (PFMS). The payment file which is sent from the MahaDBT portal to PFMS shall contain authorization of respective DDO of scheme department in the form of his DSC authentication and the same file shall be sent to State Bank of India, so that the Bank can cross check authentication and can transfer the benefit to the beneficiary’s account.
