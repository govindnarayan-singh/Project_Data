The preamble is:

Currently, for the planning and implementation of departmental schemes, a new survey is required to create a database of beneficiaries each time.

Various schemes are implemented by the Government and declared to help the citizens in the event of natural disasters. However, the implementation of these schemes has to face major challenges such as identifying eligible beneficiaries, providing assistance to them through the DBT system.

In view of the experience of various schemes successfully implemented by the Department of Information Technology across various departments and the difficulties encountered in assisting citizens during natural disasters, the implementation of Maharashtra Unified Citizen Data for Digital Delivery of Benefits and Services will be under consideration by the Government, taking into account the experience of various schemes successfully implemented by the Department of Information Technology across several departments.

Government decision:-

1. Maharashtra Unified Citizens Data for Digital Delivery of Benefits and Services
The Government has allocated Rs 294.02 crore (excluding GST) to implement the scheme.

Administrative approvals and scope, selection procedures and deadlines for project implementation are being approved.

According to the Articles, the Department of Information Technology is the custodian of citizens' data.
With this in mind, Shared Standard Citizen Database - Maharashtra Unified Citizen Data Hub
The Department of Information Technology is being authorized to produce it.

All departments and other government agencies in the state will immediately provide the computerized information of citizens available to them to the Department of Information Technology to create a common standard citizen database for the entire state. The Department of Information Technology will create a common standard citizen database, and will be the custodian of the archives in Maharashtra Unified Citizen Data Hub.

Provisions necessary for the implementation of the Sadar Scheme are included in the E-Governance Policy.

The Department of Information Technology will have the power to make provisions in the Act if needed in the future.

With the creation of the Unified Citizen Data Hub, the new system will make it mandatory for all departments of the state to onboard their individual beneficiary schemes.

After the creation of the Unified Citizen Data Hub, all individual beneficiary schemes will be unified.

Changes to Citizens' Data in the Database of Departments/Other Government Institutions will be automatically transferred to the Maharashtra Unified Citizens' Data Hub.

A Select Committee shall be constituted under the chairmanship of the Secretary-General.

The Committee will monitor and review the progress of Data Access, the implementation and benefits of services through the system, as well as resolve issues related to the implementation of the project etc.
