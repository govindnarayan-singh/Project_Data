Preamble:-

Various schemes such as National Corporation's Divyang Self-Reliance Scheme, Individual Direct Loan Scheme, eco-friendly mobile shops running on green energy (Mobile Shop on Vehicle) etc. are implemented through Maharashtra State Handicapped Finance and Development Corporation Ltd., Mumbai for persons with disabilities in the State to make them self-reliant.

2. For the financial year 2023-24, under Demand No. ZI-3, 2235 Social Security and Welfare, 02 Social Welfare, 101 Welfare of Handicapped, (05) Other Schemes for Divyang, (05) (17) Providing Eco-Friendly Vehicles to Divyang Persons to make them Self-Reliant (Mobile Shop on Vehicle) (General) (Programme) (2235 D257) 31, Grants-in-Aid (Non-Salary), a budgetary provision of Rs. 15 Crore has been made for Maharashtra State Handicapped Finance and Development Corporation Ltd. As per Circular No. 3 under reference of the Finance Department, funds up to the limit of 20% under the Budget Estimation, Allocation and Monitoring System (BEAMS) have been made available by the Finance Department for the object head Mobile Shop on Vehicle (2235 D257). The matter of releasing the funds made available on the BEAMS system to Maharashtra State Handicapped Finance and Development Corporation Ltd. was under consideration of the Government.

Government Decision:-

The Government hereby accords sanction to release a total grant of Rs.3,00,00,000/- (Rupees Three Crore Only), within the limit of 20% of the sanctioned budgetary provision, to Maharashtra State Handicapped Finance and Development Corporation Ltd. for the financial year 2023-24 under Demand No. ZI-3, 2235 Social Security and Welfare, Providing Eco-Friendly Vehicles to Divyang Persons to make them Self-Reliant (Mobile Shop on Vehicle) (General) (Programme) (2235 D257) 31, Grants-in-Aid (Non-Salary).
