Preamble:-

An independent Department of Welfare of Persons with Disabilities has been created vide General Administration Department Notification dated 15 December, 2022.

2. As per the Government Resolution at Read-2 of the General Administration Department, instructions have been given to administrative departments to constitute a Civil Services Board to make recommendations to the competent authority regarding the posting and transfer of officers/employees in Group-A, Group-B, and Group-C in government service. The matter of establishing a Civil Services Board to make recommendations to the competent authority regarding the transfer and posting of employees working at the field level subordinate to the Department of Welfare of Persons with Disabilities was under the consideration of the Government.

Government Resolution:

The Civil Services Board is hereby constituted as follows to make recommendations regarding the transfer and posting of employees belonging to Group-B (Non-Gazetted) and Group-C cadres working in field offices under the Department of Welfare of Persons with Disabilities:

Sr. No. Designation & Department Position
1 Principal Secretary / Secretary, Department of Welfare of Persons with Disabilities, Mantralaya, Mumbai Chairperson
2 Joint Secretary / Deputy Secretary, Social Justice Department, Mantralaya, Mumbai Member
3 Joint Secretary / Deputy Secretary (Di. K. 4), Department of Welfare of Persons with Disabilities, Mantralaya, Mumbai Member
4 Under Secretary / Section Officer (Di. K. 4), Department of Welfare of Persons with Disabilities, Mantralaya, Mumbai Member Secretary

2. While submitting proposals regarding transfers/postings of employees in Group-B (Non-Gazetted) and Group-C cadres, it will be necessary to take action as follows:

a) While placing proposals before the Civil Services Board (1) for transfer recommendations, it will be necessary to bring to notice the factual status regarding action as per the provisions of the Maharashtra Regulation of Transfers of Government Servants Act, 2005, as well as the background / factual position regarding proposed transfers.

b) After recommendation from the Civil Services Board (1) on the transfer proposal, it should be submitted to the competent authority for approval as per the provisions of the Maharashtra Regulation of Transfers of Government Servants Act.

c) It will be mandatory to take action on proposals related to transfers in accordance with the provisions of the Maharashtra Regulation of Transfers of Government Servants Act, 2005.
