Preamble :-

As per the directions in Paragraph No. 59 of the judicial decision of the Hon'ble Supreme Court at reference No. 1 above, as well as in connection with the hearing on the Suo Moto Petition before the Hon'ble State Human Rights Commission, the matter of lifting the restrictions under Finance Department Government Resolution dated 4th May, 2020 and urgently filling through direct recruitment the vacant posts in teaching and non-teaching cadres in aided special schools for persons with disabilities, attached hostels and training centers (workshops), children's homes for orphans and intellectually disabled persons, attached special schools and attached workshops in the State was under consideration of the Government. Accordingly, the Government has taken the following decision.

Government Resolution :-

In accordance with the Finance Department's Government Resolution dated 10th September, 2001, the High-Level Secretary Committee was established. Prior to submitting proposals for newly creating posts, declaring posts lapsed, reviving lapsed posts, etc., in government, semi-government offices, as well as state-owned enterprises, boards, aided institutions, etc.

reviving lapsed posts, etc., it is necessary to submit the proposals for approval of the High-Level Secretary Committee on the recommendation of the Sub-Committee established vide Government Resolution dated 9th June, 2017. Accordingly, vide Meeting No. 1/2023 dated 18th January, 2023 of the said Sub-Committee, relaxation has been granted as follows from the restrictions of the Finance Department Government Resolution dated 4th May, 2020 for filling up vacant posts in teaching cadre in aided special schools for persons with disabilities, training centers (workshops), children's homes for orphans and intellectually disabled persons, attached special schools, and attached workshops in the State; and vide Meeting No. 3/2023 dated 20th March, 2023 for filling up vacant posts in teaching / non-teaching cadres in the aforesaid establishments:-

1) As per the instructions in the meeting of the Sub-Committee of the Finance Department dated 18th January, 2023, relaxation has been granted from the restrictions in the Finance Department Government Resolution dated 4th May, 2020 for filling up vacant posts in the teaching cadre admissible as per the staffing pattern of Government Resolution dated 18th August, 2004 in the aforesaid establishments.

2) Approval has been granted to fill a total of 508 vacant posts on a regular basis as shown in Table "A" below in non-teaching cadres in the aforesaid establishments, and pending finalization of the revised staffing pattern for the said aided special schools / workshops and attached hostels, to fill services/posts up to a total limit of 237 manpower through external agencies as shown in Table "B" below.

B) The Commissioner, Persons with Disabilities Welfare, Pune shall verify the proposals received for vacant posts as per recruitment rules, and at the Government level, issue No-Objection Certificates school/workshop-wise to the posts found eligible during verification by 31st August, 2023.

C) After receiving the No-Objection Certificate from the Government for filling vacant posts, it will be mandatory for the concerned competent authority and NGO management institution to complete the recruitment process within three months from the date of receiving the No-Objection Certificate, following the rules laid down by the Government for direct recruitment. Therefore, the Directorate and institutions should keep proper records of the date on which No-Objection Certificates were issued to institutions.

3. In case of any irregularity in the said recruitment process, the concerned NGO management institution shall be entirely responsible.

4. It shall be the responsibility of the Commissioner, Persons with Disabilities Welfare, Pune to submit a report to the Department within one month after the concerned institution completes the recruitment process for all approved eligible posts within the stipulated time.
