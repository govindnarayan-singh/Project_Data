Preamble :-
As per the Government Resolution dated 18 August, 2004 mentioned at Read-1 above, the staffing pattern of teaching and non-teaching staff in Special Schools / Workshops and attached Hostels for Persons with Disabilities run by voluntary organizations has been fixed. Among those, 50 percent consolidated salary consisting of basic pay in the pay scale of Rs. 2550-3200 plus admissible Dearness Allowance has been sanctioned for posts filled on a contractual basis, namely Sweeper, Watchman, and Helper. Similarly, posts of Librarian, Honorary Medical Officer, Reader, and Rehabilitation Social Worker cum Employment Officer are on consolidated salary.

2. As per the Government Resolution dated 22 June, 2015 mentioned at Read-2 above, posts of Honorary Medical Officer, Vehicle Driver, and Vehicle Attendant in schools / workshops for persons with disabilities attached to Children's Homes for the Intellectually Disabled transferred from the Women and Child Development Department are on consolidated salary.

3. The matter of revising the pay / consolidated pay of the aforesaid contractual as well as consolidated salary employees was under consideration of the Government. Accordingly, the Government has taken the following decision.

Government Resolution :-

Therefore, for the employees in the posts of Sweeper, Watchman and Helper in Special Schools / Workshops and attached Hostels for Persons with Disabilities run by voluntary organizations whose salary is less than the minimum wage of the “Unskilled” category shown in the above table, the salary of such employees should be fixed as per the rates admissible to the offices in the zone under whose area the concerned establishment falls. Also, Dearness Allowance is declared by the Labour Commissioner twice a year for the period January to June and July to December. Accordingly, the recalculation of total minimum wage (Basic Pay + Dearness Allowance) should be done twice a year.
