Government Circular:-

To resolve the grievances of disabled brothers and sisters of various categories at the district level, officers of all departments of the government and all government mechanisms shall remain present at one place, hear the grievances of persons with disabilities, and dispose of the work at the very same location. For this purpose, as per the Government Resolution of this department dated 23 May 2023, the campaign "Department of Persons with Disabilities Welfare - Persons with Disabilities at Doorsteps" is being implemented. The campaign will be organized district-wise across all districts in the State as per the attached "Form B" schedule. Accordingly, for effective implementation of this campaign at the district level, adopting a general Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) will ensure maximum utility. Therefore, while implementing the "Department of Persons with Disabilities Welfare - Persons with Disabilities at Doorsteps" campaign at the district level, the following general standard procedure should be adopted.

A) Pre-campaign Preparation:- The following actions should be taken under this:

As per the Government Resolution dated 23 May 2023, instructions were issued that the date and other details of the campaign program to be held in each district should be finalized by the concerned District Collector from the State Level Committee. However, the Chief Guide of the campaign, Hon'ble Shri Omprakash alias Bachchu Kadu, Minister-level rank, has finalized the district-wise dates as per "Form B". Accordingly, a meeting of the District Level Committee constituted as per the above Government Resolution should be held to make complete preparations for this drive. In this meeting, matters regarding persons with disabilities under the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act should be discussed and generally decisions should be taken regarding the following matters:

  1. Finalizing the location for the event.
  2. After finalizing the location for the campaign program, to maximize participation of disabled brothers and sisters, news/information about the campaign should be broadcast/published through local newspapers and FM radio.
  3. As it is currently the rainy season, large halls should be arranged for the program.
  4. The building and premises of the program should be accessible to persons with disabilities.
  5. The District Collector should convene a meeting of all Department Heads in the district and guide them regarding their duties and responsibilities.
  6. Stalls should be designated for each department. (As per "Form A")
  7. The list of various stalls (a list of guiding stalls is attached) should be finalized considering local conditions.
  8. A list of Grievance Redressal Officers under Section 22 of the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act across various departments should be compiled, and a review of currently pending applications of persons with disabilities should be taken.
  9. To ensure maximum persons with disabilities benefit from the campaign, their (preliminary) online registration should be conducted, and information as well as demands should be compiled.
  10. To reach maximum disabled beneficiaries in the district, Asha Workers and Anganwadi Workers should collect filled application forms for required schemes from disabled beneficiaries in their respective areas.
  11. Applications of disabled beneficiaries received from Asha Workers and Anganwadi Workers should be classified and forwarded to the respective concerned departments.
  12. Applications of persons with disabilities received by concerned departments from Asha Workers and Anganwadi Workers, as well as pending applications of disabled beneficiaries previously lying with concerned departments, should be processed prior to the event, and planning should be made to distribute approved benefits/certificates to beneficiaries on the day of the campaign event.
  13. A review regarding the 5% reserved fund for persons with disabilities in local self-government bodies (rural and urban) should be taken, and planning should be made on how this fund can be utilized for this campaign.
  14. A review of the implementation of reservation for persons with disabilities in government recruitment across various establishments at the district level should be taken, and action should be initiated to fill backlog.
  15. A 5% reservation for persons with disabilities has been fixed in various government schools, colleges, and other educational institutions. A review should be conducted regarding this, and its implementation ensured.
  16. At all stalls, Sign Language Interpreters should be appointed to understand the grievances of deaf and mute disabled persons and to provide them information.
  17. Reviewing the 2011 disabled population of the district and pending cases of Unique Disability ID (UDID CARD) as of 30th March, 2023, and new application cases, action should be taken to examine maximum persons with disabilities and issue Unique Disability IDs and Disability Certificates.

B) Duties and Responsibilities of Department Heads :-

  1. Every Department Head shall outline how the beneficiaries with disabilities can avail the benefits of their department's schemes on the day of the campaign program, present it in the meeting chaired by the District Collector, and finalize it.
  2. Every department should publish news in local newspapers and generate necessary public awareness so that disabled beneficiaries get information about the required documents for their schemes, so that disabled beneficiaries will attend the campaign program with the necessary documents on the event day and submit applications to avail scheme benefits. If possible, the District Collector and Chief Executive Officer (Zilla Parishad) should broadcast information through their interviews on All India Radio (Akashvani).
  3. Every department should print adequate copies of information booklets/leaflets/pamphlets of their schemes and distribute them to disabled beneficiaries on the program day.
  4. QR codes for various schemes for persons with disabilities as well as Government Resolutions and e-books should be prepared, and information and publicity regarding this should be done at the venue.

C) Format of the Program :-

  1. On the day of the campaign program, vehicle facilities should be provided to transport disabled beneficiaries arriving at the district location from the bus stand to the program venue.
  2. After disabled beneficiaries arrive at the program venue, registration of every disabled beneficiary should be carried out. For this purpose, facilities and manpower should be made available category-wise according to disability type. Sufficient number of registration counters should be made available so that overcrowding does not occur at one spot. Priority should be given to computerised online registration methods.
  3. To enable disabled beneficiaries to apply for various schemes of different departments, each department should make forms in the prescribed format available to beneficiaries on the day of the event.
  4. Applications for government schemes have to be submitted manually as well as online for certain schemes. For this purpose, on the day of the program, if a disabled person wishing to avail benefits at any department's stall is not capable of filling out the manual/online form, volunteers as well as computers and computer operators should be provided to assist in filling the form. The responsibility of providing computers, computer operators, and volunteers in adequate proportion lies with the respective department.
  5. Applications of disabled beneficiaries received from Asha Workers and Anganwadi Workers, as well as pending applications of disabled persons previously lying with departments, should be processed prior to the program, and eligible benefits/certificates to be given by the department should be distributed to the concerned beneficiaries on the day of the event.
  6. The concerned department shall take prompt action on priority basis regarding applications of schemes received on the day of the program, and on the day of the program, a firm guarantee should be given to the beneficiary regarding the timeframe within which they will receive the benefit of the filled scheme application.

  1. Every department should provide information about their schemes to disabled beneficiaries present at the program. For this purpose, printed pamphlets/leaflets should be distributed by departments.
    (1) Or by providing QR Code availability, Government Resolutions containing information on pamphlets/leaflets should be made available to everyone.
    (2) District Collectors should make doctors from District Health Department and District Medical College available, and if possible, organize a check-up camp to check and register for Unique Disability IDs.
  2. The D.D.R.C. of the district should distribute artificial limbs and appliances on the program day as per applications received by them.
  3. Non-Governmental Organizations distributing artificial limbs and appliances, C.R.C. or D.D.R.C., should take measurements of disabled beneficiaries as per applications received on the event day and prepare beneficiary-wise records regarding the required artificial limbs and appliances to be provided.
  4. Arrangements for drinking water, tea/refreshments, and meals should be made for disabled beneficiaries on the day of the program.
  5. Wheelchairs should be made available at the program venue as per requirement for easy movement of persons with disabilities.
  6. Disability helpers/staff should be appointed so that disabled beneficiaries do not face difficulty going to each department stall, with whose assistance disabled beneficiaries can reach the required department stalls.
  7. Xerox machines/scanners/printers should be kept available at the venue so that these facilities can be provided to persons with disabilities on a payment basis.

D) Post-Campaign Action:-

  1. Departments should maintain separate records regarding the benefits distributed as well as applications received on the day of the campaign program.
  2. Every department should process applications received on the day of the program on top priority and immediately, provide required benefits to eligible beneficiaries, and communicate the action taken on each application to the concerned beneficiary.
  3. In this context, all Department Heads should review their respective departments and take action on priority.
  4. The action taken on applications received during the program should be reviewed in the District Level Committee meeting under the chairmanship of the District Collector.

E) The district-wise dates of the campaign have been indicated in the attached "Form B" as per the convenience of the Chief Guide of the campaign, Hon'ble Shri Omprakash alias Bachchu Kadu. Therefore, all districts should take action as above.
