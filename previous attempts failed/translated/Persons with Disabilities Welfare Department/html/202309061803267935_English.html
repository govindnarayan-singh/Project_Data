Preamble :-

As per the Government Resolution of Finance Department dated 8th May, 2014, in accordance with Rule 56, Clause 3 of the Maharashtra Contingent Expenditure Rules, there is a provision that Administrative Departments shall sanction permanent advance up to Rs. 5000/- for the offices of Heads of Departments without consulting the Accountant General. In accordance therewith, approval was granted to sanction a permanent advance of Rs. 5000/- for urgent official work of the newly created Divyang Kalyan Department vide Memorandum of even number dated 16th June, 2023 of the Department. Presently, the said department is functioning in rented premises at Mittal Tower, "A" Wing, Nariman Point, Mumbai, and expenses have to be incurred by the department from time to time for refreshments / tea served for meetings organized in connection with various subjects. As the amount of permanent advance of Rs. 5000/- falls short for this purpose, the matter of increasing the said amount to Rs. 10,000/- was under the consideration of the department.

Government Memorandum :-

A proposal was submitted to the Finance Department seeking approval to increase the permanent advance amount for Divyang Kalyan Department (Proper) from Rs. 5000/- to Rs. 10,000/-. Thereon, approval was granted to fix the amount of permanent advance in consultation with the Office of the Accountant General vide Finance Department's Unofficial Reference No.: 250 / 2023 / Viniyam, dated 6 July, 2023. Accordingly, upon submitting the said proposal to the Accountant General (Accounts & Entitlement)-I, Maharashtra, Mumbai, approval has been granted to increase the permanent advance amount from Rs. 5000/- to Rs. 10,000/- vide their office Letter No.: TM / Ch. 5 / Persons with Disabilities Welfare Dept / UOR-81 / 617, dated 1st September, 2023.
