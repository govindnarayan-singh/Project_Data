Preamble:-

For the financial year 2023-24, as per the circular under reference No. 1 of the Finance Department, under the main head of account Demand No. ZI-3, 2235, Social Security and Welfare under the Divyang Welfare Department for the Maharashtra State Divyang Finance and Development Corporation Ltd., Mumbai, funds under mandatory schemes under the detailed head 36-Grants-in-aid (Salary) within the limit of 30% have been disbursed on the Budget Estimation, Allocation and Monitoring System (BEAMS). As per the Government Resolution under reference No. 3, funds amounting to Rs. 19.10 Lakh have been disbursed to the Corporation. The Managing Director, Maharashtra State Divyang Finance and Development Corporation Ltd., Mumbai, vide letter under reference No. 4, submitted a proposal to the Government for disbursement of funds from the approved budget provision under 36-Grants-in-aid (Salary) for the mandatory schemes of the Corporation. Accordingly, the matter of disbursing the funds was under the consideration of the Government.

Government Resolution:-

Various loan schemes are implemented through Maharashtra State Divyang Finance and Development Corporation Ltd., Mumbai to provide financial assistance in the form of loans for self-employment to persons with disabilities (Divyang) in the State.

For the financial year 2023-24, under Demand No. ZI-3, 2235, Social Security and Welfare (Mandatory) (2235D364), under the detailed head 36-Grants-in-aid (Salary) within the 30% limit of the budgetary provision, sanction of the Government is hereby accorded for the disbursement of a total grant of Rs. 26,80,720/- (Rupees Twenty-Six Lakhs Eighty Thousand Seven Hundred Twenty Only). 2. The grant under this Government Resolution is being disbursed subject to the following terms and conditions:

  1. Before drawing the funds available on the Budget Distribution System (BEAMS) from the Treasury, the guiding instructions mentioned in paragraphs 4 to 18 of the Finance Department circular dated 12.04.2023 and the guiding instructions in the Appendix should be strictly implemented.

  1. The funds disbursed scheme-wise / non-scheme-wise and item-wise to the Corporation should be spent only for admissible items under the concerned mandatory scheme.
  2. Smooth functioning should be brought into operations with a view to keeping administrative expenditure within limits.
  3. Detailed information of amounts spent on various items, expenditure incurred thereon, as well as information regarding unspent funds of the previous year should be submitted to the Government immediately.
  4. Every effort should be made by the Corporation to effectively implement loan recovery.
  5. Expenditure should not be incurred merely because budget provision exists or grant has been disbursed. Economy should be observed in office expenditure.

3. Regarding this administrative grant, the Deputy Secretary (Corporations), Divyang Welfare-3, Divyang Welfare Department, Mantralaya, Mumbai shall be the Controlling Officer. The amount of the said grant is being placed at the disposal of the Regional Deputy Commissioner, Social Welfare, Navi Mumbai for further action. As per Finance Department, G.R. No. Mandivi-2022/C.R.24/2022/Rules, dated 12.01.2023, the said grant is unconditional.
