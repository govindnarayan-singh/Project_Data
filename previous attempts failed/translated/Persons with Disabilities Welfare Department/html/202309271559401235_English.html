Preamble :-

As per the Government Resolution dated 10.06.2019 cited under reference No. 1, a decision was taken to provide environment-friendly mobile shops on vehicles running on green energy (Mobile Shop on Vehicle) to persons with disabilities to enable them to become self-reliant. Under this scheme, a maximum grant of Rs. 3.75 lakh is to be provided to disabled beneficiaries for mobile shops on vehicles. As per Para 4, a Selection Committee has been constituted to select the disabled beneficiaries. In this committee, the Manager, Handicapped Finance and Development Corporation, Regional Office, Nagpur was appointed as a member; however, as the said office has been closed since 27.08.2018, it is necessary to reconstitute the committee. The matter of modifying the composition of the committee as well as amending the terms and conditions for the selection of beneficiaries was under the consideration of the Government.

Corrigendum :-

In Para 4 of the Government Resolution cited under reference No. 1 regarding providing environment-friendly mobile shops on vehicles running on green energy to persons with disabilities to enable them to become self-reliant, instead of "Regional Manager, Handicapped Finance and Development Corporation, Mumbai Regional Office, Nagpur - Member",

Government Corrigendum No.: DIVIM-2023/C.R.09/Divyang Kalyan-3

it should be read as "Accounts Officer, Persons with Disabilities Finance and Development Corporation, Mumbai - Member".

Since the Regional Office, Nagpur of the Persons with Disabilities Finance and Development Corporation has been closed, eligible applications should be submitted directly through the District Manager to the Headquarters of the Persons with Disabilities Corporation.

In Para 6 "Terms and Conditions of the Scheme", after Sr. No. 9:

10. The applicant should not be an employee of Government / Semi-Government / Boards / Corporations.
11. If the applicant applying for this scheme is a borrower of the Persons with Disabilities Finance and Development Corporation, he/she should not be a defaulter.

These conditions are being included.

2. As per the Government Circular dated 04.07.2019, since the word "Divyang" (Disabled) is used instead of the word "Apang" (Handicapped), the name of Handicapped Corporation should now be read as "Persons with Disabilities Finance and Development Corporation Ltd., Mumbai", and necessary amendments should be made accordingly.

3. For the implementation of the Mobile Shop on Vehicle scheme, necessary cooperation for the selected business will be extended to disabled beneficiaries by the Housing Department, Urban Development Department, and Rural Development Department.
