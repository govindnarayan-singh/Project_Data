Preamble:-

Along with the effective implementation of various schemes launched for the economic, social, educational, and cultural development of persons with disabilities, an independent Persons with Disabilities Welfare Department was created by the Government Notification under Reference No. 1 of the General Administration Department for their overall development. Under Reference No. 2 Government Resolution dated 05 October, 2023, approval was granted to continue 353 temporary posts under the Commissioner, Persons with Disabilities Welfare, Pune from 01.09.2023 to 29.02.2024. In the financial year 2024-25, the matter of granting extension to 350 temporary posts on the establishment of the Commissionerate for Persons with Disabilities Welfare, Pune from 01.03.2024 to 31.08.2024 was under the consideration of the Government.

Government Resolution:-

Sanction is hereby accorded to extend 350 temporary posts on the establishment of the Commissioner, Persons with Disabilities Welfare, Pune, from 01.03.2024 to 31.08.2024, subject to the following terms and conditions:-

  1. Vacant posts exceeding six months, excluding the post of Head of Office, should be excluded.
  2. Subject to the condition that temporary posts other than executive posts have not remained vacant for more than six months, the said extension is being granted.
  3. The terms and conditions subject to which the temporary posts were sanctioned for specific reasons must be complied with.
  4. As and when officers/employees on supernumerary posts are absorbed into other posts, such supernumerary posts shall automatically stand abolished from the date of absorption.
