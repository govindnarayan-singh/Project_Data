Government Circular :-

In order to resolve the difficulties of persons with disabilities belonging to various categories, officers from all government departments and all government machineries can remain present at a single location at the district level to hear the grievances of persons with disabilities and dispose of the work on the spot. For this purpose, as per the Government Resolution of this Department dated 23 May 2023, the campaign "Persons with Disabilities Welfare Department at the Doorstep of Persons with Disabilities" is being implemented, and the procedure to be followed while implementing the said campaign at the district level has been detailed vide the Department's Government Circular dated 28 July 2023. In "Form B" attached with the said circular, the schedule of the campaign to be organized in all districts has been given.
