Official warrants

According to the Government Decree of the Ministry of Finance dated March 2, 2019, the three-benefit Progress Scheme under Improved Services, after 10, 20 and 30 years of regular service, has been implemented from January 1, 2016 for State Government employees during the term of the 7th Pay Commission. The beneficiaries of the three-benefit scheme in accordance with paragraph 4 of the Sadhar Government Decree are required to have completed the qualification, seniority, eligibility, eligibility examination, departmental examination, validation of confidential report, validity of the case of departmental inquiry, validity of the case of justice, availability of certificates such as the promotion, promotion of the functional division as per the post of promotion.

The Department of Disability Welfare has been re-created by the Department of General Administration with the notification dated December 15, 2022 and the Department Promotion Committee is being constituted as set out in the table below as per the provisions of the above Governance Decree to recommend the qualified officers/staff of the Department to benefit from the aforementioned Promise Progress Scheme.
