Government circular:

Various schemes/Jobs for the Disabled implemented by the Central/State Government

Detailed instructions are being issued regarding mandatory Universal ID Card (UDID Card) for persons with disabilities in the State to benefit from reservations / promotions / discounts etc.

2.

1)

Universal ID Card for Disabled Persons (UDID Card) in the State is being made mandatory to benefit from various schemes/discounts for disabled persons implemented by the Union/State Government and for those who do not have any Disability Certificate, it is mandatory for them to obtain a Disability Certificate and Universal ID Card (UDID Card) through the Medical Board established from time to time by the Department of Public Health (Department of Public Health) on 14th September, 2018.

Persons with disabilities who have a disability certificate issued by the competent authority appointed by the Government before the Public Health Department decision dated 14th September, 2018 are required to obtain a disability certificate and a Global ID Card (UDID Card) through the Medical Board established by the said Government decision.

Individuals with disabilities who do not have a disability certificate issued by the competent authorities designated by the Government from time to time, but have other medical/diligence certificates issued by Government hospitals and on the basis of which they benefit from discounts/schemes for disabled persons, such persons with disabilities will be required to obtain a disability certificate/universal certificate (UDID) from the Medical Board established from time to time as per the Government Decree of the Department of Public Health dated 14th September, 2018 for a period of one year from the date of the Government Circular.
