The preamble -

The Maharashtra Public Service Right Act, 2015 was enacted on 28.4.2015 to ensure the provision of public services to qualified persons in the State through the State Government in a transparent, efficient and timely manner.

In accordance with the provisions of Section 3 of the Maharashtra Public Service Rights Act, 2015, various services provided to persons with disabilities under the Department of Disability Welfare have been made available from time to time under the dates 2, 3 and 4 of the Reference Sector Offices. At a meeting held on Friday, 08.11.2004, under the Chairmanship of the State Main Service Right Commission, the Commission has directed the Chairperson of the Department's Senior Consulting Committee for Business Process Re-Engineering to be appointed as the Chief Consulting Officer for the Department of Disability Welfare, to notify the Department of disabled persons under the Department of Disability Welfare, and to notify the Department of disabled persons under the provisions of the Department of Disability Welfare.
