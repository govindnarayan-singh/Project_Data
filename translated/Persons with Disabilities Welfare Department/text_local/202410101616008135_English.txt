The aim of the Government is to empower the disabled person by planning various initiatives and schemes for the overall development of persons with disabilities (education, training, rehabilitation, financial assistance, social security, positive action plan) and to bring them into the mainstream of society. Accordingly, the Cabinet Meeting dated 29.11.2022 decided to create a 2063 posts at the level of the Department of Social Justice and Special Branch to handle the functions of the Department of Disabled Welfare by creating an Administrative Division, the Department of Administrative Division (DDA), the Commissioner's level, the Regional level, the District level and the District level.
