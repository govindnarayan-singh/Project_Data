Government decision:-

Measures in place to ensure the safety of students entering government/subsidized residential/non-resident special schools, workshops, orphanages under the Department of Disability Welfare should be effectively implemented along with the following measures: (a) on the installation and maintenance of CCTV cameras (CCTV) in the premises of schools, government and subsidized hostels under the Department:-

1. CCTV cameras should be installed within the next 15 days from the date of this Government decision to ensure the safety of students, teachers and non-teaching staff entering residential residences in government/subsidized/non-residential special schools, workshops, orphanages under the Department of Disability Welfare.
