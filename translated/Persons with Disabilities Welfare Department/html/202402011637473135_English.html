Reference:- Minutes of the meeting held on 22nd November, 2023 under the Chairmanship of Hon. Chief Minister.

Preamble:-

As per the minutes of the meeting held on 22nd November, 2023 under the chairmanship of Hon. Chief Minister, a decision was taken to establish a committee to create an automated system instead of providing assistants / scribes / readers to disabled candidates in examinations. Accordingly, the matter of establishing the committee was under the consideration of the Government.

Government Resolution:-

To create a technology-based automated system instead of providing assistants / scribes / readers to disabled candidates in examinations, a committee is hereby constituted as follows:

  1. Additional Chief Secretary / Principal Secretary / Secretary, Persons with Disabilities Welfare Department - Chairman
  2. Additional Chief Secretary / Principal Secretary / Secretary, (Information and Technology) General Administration Department - Member
  3. Commissioner, Persons with Disabilities Welfare - Member Secretary

2. The said committee should submit its report within one month.
