Government Circular :-

In pursuance of the order dated 16th February, 2023 passed by the Hon'ble High Court, Bench at Aurangabad in Writ Petition No. 1881/2023 and other petitions, directions have been issued vide Finance Department Circular under Reference No. 2 above instructing all Ministry Administrative Departments to inform the Heads of Departments in their subordinate offices to appeal to all concerned retirees who retired on 30th June after rendering qualifying service for the preceding 12 months, to submit applications to the respective offices for revising their pension by considering the notional increment as on 01st July. Similarly, directions have also been issued through the said circular that after receiving such applications, the concerned offices, while granting revised pensionary benefits to the concerned persons as mentioned in the order under Reference No. 1 above of the Hon'ble High Court, Bench at Aurangabad, shall pay arrears for a period of preceding 3 years from the date of filing the application or from the date of their retirement, whichever is less, and that no application of any retiree fulfilling the guidelines laid down by the Hon'ble High Court shall be rejected.

2. Therefore, in pursuance of the above-mentioned Circular dated 28th June, 2023 of the Finance Department, the increment as on 1st July is also being made admissible to those employees / officers in Special Schools / Workshops for Persons with Disabilities / Homes for Intellectually Disabled Children who have retired on 30th June or will retire in the future. The Commissioner, Persons with Disabilities Welfare, Pune is hereby informed to bring this to the notice of Special Schools / Workshops for Persons with Disabilities / Homes for Intellectually Disabled Children in the State from his level and issue directions to take appropriate action as per the referenced Government Circular.

3. This Government Circular is being issued in accordance with the approval granted vide Finance Department's Unofficial Reference No. 444/(Seva-3), dated 22nd November, 2023.
