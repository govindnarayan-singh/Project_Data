Preamble :-

Sanmati Mentally Retarded School, Dhule, a special school run by Late Ratibai Maganlal Jain Public Trust, Dhule, holds Registration Certificate No. 0217 dated 06.01.1999, which has been renewed up to 02.04.2024. The matter of transferring the management of the said functioning aided special school for persons with disabilities, Sanmati Mentally Retarded School, Dhule, run by Late Ratibai Maganlal Jain Public Trust, Dhule to Shri Radhegovind Bahuuddeshiya Sanstha, Dhule, Taluka & District Dhule, was under consideration of the Government. Accordingly, the Government has taken the following decision.

Government Resolution :-

Approval is hereby granted by this Government Resolution to transfer the management of Sanmati Mentally Retarded School, Dhule, a functioning aided special school for persons with disabilities run by Late Ratibai Maganlal Jain Public Trust, Dhule, to Shri Radhegovind Bahuuddeshiya Sanstha, Dhule, Taluka & District Dhule.

2. The existing rules of the scheme for Recognition and Grant-in-aid for Special Schools for Persons with Disabilities and the rules that come into force from time to time shall be binding on the said institution.

3. The entire process regarding the transfer of management of the said special school shall be completed within a period of three months, and a report in this regard shall be submitted to the Government by the Commissioner, Persons with Disabilities Welfare, Pune.

4. Within 30 days after the issuance of the management transfer order, it shall be mandatory for the said institution to obtain a registration certificate from the Competent Authority and Commissioner, Persons with Disabilities Welfare, Pune, under the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016.

4. All expenses related to the transfer of management of the said special school shall be borne by the concerned institution. No reimbursement of the said expenses shall be admissible.

5. The institution taking over the management of the subject school shall absorb all recognized and working employees of the original school into their original posts after the proposed transfer.

6. The institution taking over the management of the subject school shall run the said school at its original location.
