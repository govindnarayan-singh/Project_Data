OFFICE ORDER

In accordance with Rules 2 and 7 under Part I, Sub-Department 2 of the Financial Power Rules Manual, 1978, under Appendix-4 to Rule-19 of the Maharashtra Contingent Expenditure Rules, 1965, the administrative department has been conferred powers to incur expenditure up to Rs. 5 Lakhs per annum on contingent items for its own office. Under condition No. 3 of the said rule, a provision has been made that the administrative department may re-delegate these powers to the In-charge Deputy Secretary / Joint Secretary of the Administration Branch of the concerned office. Pursuant to the said provision, the financial powers of the said administrative department are hereby re-delegated to the In-charge Joint / Deputy Secretary, Administration Branch, Persons with Disabilities Welfare Department, Mantralaya.
