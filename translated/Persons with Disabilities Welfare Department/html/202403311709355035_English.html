Preamble:-

In order to identify the problems/needs of persons with disabilities and to rehabilitate them comprehensively so as to bring them into the mainstream of society, sanction was accorded vide Government Resolution under Reference No. 2 to conduct a door-to-door survey of persons with disabilities in the State for planning schemes, programs, projects for persons with disabilities, collecting data for effective implementation, and collecting data for research regarding persons with disabilities. For this, initially, the survey work has been completed in Akola, Parbhani, Satara districts of the State, and as the survey work is ongoing in Beed, Dhule and Thane districts, excluding these 6 districts, funds amounting to Rs. 25 Lakh per district were sanctioned to start the survey work for the remaining 30 districts. The matter of releasing funds of total Rs. 17.50 Crore, comprising the said Rs. 7.50 Crore funds and remaining Rs. 10 Crore within the limit of revised budget estimates, was under consideration of the Government.

Government Decision:-

For conducting the survey of persons with disabilities in the State, the survey work has been completed in Akola, Parbhani, Satara districts, and as the survey work is ongoing in Beed, Dhule and Thane districts, excluding the said 6 districts, sanction is hereby accorded vide this Government Resolution to release funds amounting to Rs. 25 Lakh per district totaling Rs. 7.50 Crore as sanctioned earlier for the survey work of the remaining 30 districts, and additionally, within the limit of revised budget estimates, Rs. 52 Lakh each for Mumbai City and Mumbai Suburban districts and Rs. 32 Lakh per district for the remaining 28 districts, totaling Rs. 10 Crore as additional funds, thus releasing a total fund of Rs. 17,50,00,000/- (Rupees Seventeen Crore Fifty Lakh only).

2. Before withdrawing the funds made available on the Budget Distribution System from the treasury, the guidance instructions and guidance instructions in the appendix specified in the circulars of the Finance Department dated 12.04.2023 and dated 04.03.2024 should be strictly implemented.
