CIRCULAR

In connection with filling up vacant posts in the teaching cadre permissible as per the staffing pattern of Government Resolution dated 18th August, 2004 in the teaching and non-teaching cadres of aided special schools, attached hostels, workshops (training centres) for persons with disabilities and children's homes for orphan intellectually disabled persons in the State, a Government Circular of even number dated 31st October, 2023 has been issued regarding terms and conditions. In the said Government Circular dated 31st October, 2023, condition No. 15 "xv. Care should be taken to comply with the provisions in the Government Resolution of the Finance Department dated 27.04.2022 while filling vacant posts to be filled through outsourcing in disability schemes mentioned in the Government Resolution dated 18.08.2004 of the Social Justice and Special Assistance Department and Government Resolution No. Petition-2021/C.R.13/Dis.Welf.-1, dated 26.07.2023." as well as condition No. 16 "xvi. Compliance with the above-mentioned conditions as well as conditions prescribed from time to time by the Government regarding recruitment of posts shall be binding on the concerned." are being included.
