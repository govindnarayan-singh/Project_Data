  1. It shall be mandatory for all three schools to implement the rules applied by the Government from time to time.
  2. For recruitment of teaching and non-teaching posts, it shall be mandatory to fill posts as per Government Resolution No. EDD-2018/C.R.288/A.K.-1, Dated 18 July, 2018 of Social Justice and Special Assistance Department and Government Corrigendum No. EDD-2018/C.R.288/A.K.-1, Dated 5 February, 2019 of Social Justice and Special Assistance Department and service recruitment rules. Otherwise, the said recruitment process shall be deemed illegal. Also, legal action will be taken against the concerned as per rules. However, if the institution issues appointment orders by bypassing the prescribed process and the concerned District Social Welfare Officer, Zilla Parishad / Assistant Commissioner, Mumbai City/Suburbs grants approval to it, then the concerned District Social Welfare Officer, Assistant Commissioner, Mumbai City/Suburbs shall be held fully responsible.
  3. While conducting recruitment for teaching and non-teaching posts, reservation must be got fixed from the Backward Class Cell as per the new reservation policy, and recruitment must be conducted only as per the roster finalized after getting such reservation determined.
  4. For recruitment to teaching and non-teaching cadres, reservation must be determined by the Backward Class Cell. Posts with such determined reservation should be filled strictly according to the roster. Recruitment shall be conducted only after verifying category-wise social reservation, Economically Weaker Section (EWS) reservation apart from social reservation, and horizontal reservation (Women, Sports Persons, Persons with Disabilities, etc.).
  5. While filling teaching posts, the provisions prescribed in Social Justice and Special Assistance Department Government Resolution No. EDD-2018/C.R.288/A.K.-1, Dated 18 July, 2018 and Social Justice and Special Assistance Department Government Corrigendum No. EDD-2018/C.R.288/A.K.-1, Dated 5 February, 2019 should be taken into consideration, and mandatory educational qualification/eligibility for the posts mentioned in Social Justice and Special Assistance Department Government Resolution No. EDD-2003/C.R.340/Part-3/Reform-2, Dated 18 August, 2004 and Government Resolutions issued by the Government from time to time must be strictly followed.
  6. While appointing to these posts, selection should be made through the prescribed Selection Committee constituted under the Government Resolution as per the prevailing law. Recruitment conducted without following the prescribed procedure shall be cancelled, and if any court case arises in future regarding appointments, the entire responsibility for the same shall lie with the concerned Institution, District Social Welfare Officer / Assistant Commissioner, Mumbai City/Suburbs.
  7. For the selection process of teaching and non-teaching posts, the responsibility of determining the eligibility of candidates considering the provisions in the prevailing rules shall lie with the concerned Institution. Also, if appointments are given by bypassing prevailing rules, the concerned District Social Welfare Officer / Assistant Commissioner, Mumbai City/Suburbs / Commissioner, Persons with Disabilities Welfare, and the concerned Institution / Principal / Management shall be held fully responsible.
  8. While filling teaching and non-teaching posts, recruitment must be conducted by strictly adhering to the qualifications, experience, and professional eligibility prescribed by the Government of Maharashtra from time to time.
