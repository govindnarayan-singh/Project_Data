Preamble:-

The registration of Maharashtra State Handicapped Finance and Development Corporation has been done under Section 25 of the Companies Act, 1956. At the time of establishment of the Corporation, the authorized share capital of the Corporation was sanctioned at Rs. 50 crore. Vide Government Resolution dated 08.06.2022, the authorized share capital of the Corporation has been increased from Rs. 50 crore to Rs. 500 crore. Out of the share capital made available from time to time to the Corporation, financial assistance in the form of loans is provided under self-employment loan schemes for disabled persons in the state through the Corporation. Also, this Corporation acts as the State Channelising Agency for the National Handicapped Finance and Development Corporation, New Delhi.

2. A budgetary provision of Rs. 90.21 Lakh under 31 Grants-in-aid (Non-Salary) has been sanctioned under mandatory expenditure for the financial year 2023-24 under Demand No. ZI-3, 2235 Social Security and Welfare, 02 Social Welfare, 101 Welfare of Handicapped, (08) (01) Managerial Grant to Maharashtra State Handicapped Finance and Development Corporation (2235 D364) for Maharashtra State Handicapped Finance and Development Corporation. Vide Government Resolutions at Reference Nos. 3 and 4 of the Department, a total fund of Rs. 18.04 Lakh has been distributed to the Corporation. Out of the sanctioned non-salary grant to the Corporation, expenditure was incurred through the Corporation for honorarium of outsourced service employees, other office expenses i.e. stationery, furniture, office building rent, other pending expenses at headquarters, expenditure incurred for the event of World Disability Day under the chairmanship of Hon'ble Chief Minister on 03.12.2022, as well as office expenses of this Department as the Persons with Disabilities Welfare Department is newly created. In the financial year 2023-24, under budget head 2235 D364, 31, Grants-in-aid (Non-Salary), since the fund was very minimal compared to the expenditure, a supplementary demand was submitted to the Finance Department to meet the remaining expenditure. The Finance Department, from the sanctioned grant of the current financial year...

...as it was possible to meet the expenditure, a token supplementary demand of Rs. 1,000/- was presented, and a fund of Rs. 20.00 Lakh was sanctioned under Item No. 142 in the second (Budgetary) session of February, 2024 of the State Legislature. The matter of distributing the fund sanctioned through supplementary demand to the Maharashtra State Handicapped Finance and Development Corporation is under consideration of the Government.

Government Resolution:-

Under budget head (2235 D364), 31 Grants-in-aid (Non-Salary), a budgetary provision of Rs. 90.21 Lakh under mandatory expenditure was sanctioned for the financial year 2023-24 for the Maharashtra State Handicapped Finance and Development Corporation. Since the funds under budget head 2235 D364, 31, Grants-in-aid (Non-Salary) sanctioned to the Corporation were insufficient compared to the expenditure, Government sanction is hereby accorded to disburse funds of Rs. 20,00,000/- (Rupees Twenty Lakh only) sanctioned under Item No. 142 in the second (Budgetary) session of February, 2024 of the State Legislature through supplementary demand to make funds available to the Corporation, to the Maharashtra State Handicapped Finance and Development Corporation as per this Government Resolution.

2. Before drawing the funds available on the Budget Distribution System (BDS) from the treasury, the guidelines specified in the Finance Department's circulars dated 12.04.2023 and dated 04.03.2024 and the guidelines in the appendix should be strictly implemented.

4. The Deputy Secretary (Disabilities Welfare-2), Persons with Disabilities Welfare Department, Mantralaya, Mumbai shall be the Controlling Officer for these funds.
