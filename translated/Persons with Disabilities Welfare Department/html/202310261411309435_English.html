Preamble :-
The matter regarding the transfer of Pratignya Residential School for Persons with Disabilities, M.I.D.C. Latur, Taluka & District Latur, run by Marathwada Apang Sanghatana, Latur, which has been closed since the year 2006, to Dr. Babasaheb Ambedkar Sevabhavi Sanstha, Shambhargaon, Post Martala, Taluka Loha, District Nanded, and its relocation to Bondhar Haveli, Taluka & District Nanded, was under consideration of the Government. Accordingly, the Government has taken the following decision.

Government Resolution :-
By this Government Resolution, sanction is hereby accorded, with the approval of the Hon’ble Chief Minister as a special case, for the transfer of the closed special school belonging to the orthopedically handicapped category, namely Pratignya Residential School for Persons with Disabilities, M.I.D.C. Latur, Taluka & District Latur, run by Marathwada Apang Sanghatana, Latur, to Dr. Babasaheb Ambedkar Sevabhavi Sanstha, Shambhargaon, Post Martala, Taluka Loha, District Nanded, and for its relocation to Bondhar Haveli, Taluka & District Nanded.

2. The existing rules as well as rules coming into existence from time to time under the scheme of recognition and grant-in-aid for special schools for persons with disabilities shall be binding on the said institution.

3. All procedures regarding the relocation and transfer of the said special school should be completed within a period of three months; otherwise, the said order shall be deemed to be cancelled. The Commissioner, Persons with Disabilities Welfare, Pune shall submit a report in this regard to the Government.

4. Within 30 days after the issuance of the transfer and relocation order, it shall be mandatory for the said institution to obtain a registration certificate under the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016 from the Competent Authority and Commissioner, Persons with Disabilities Welfare, Pune.

4. All expenses for the transfer and relocation of the said special school will have to be borne by the concerned institution. Reimbursement for the said expenditure will not be admissible.

5. The institution taking over the subject school shall absorb all recognized and working employees of the original school into their original posts after the proposed transfer and relocation.
