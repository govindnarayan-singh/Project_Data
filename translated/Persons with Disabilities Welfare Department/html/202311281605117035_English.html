Preamble :-
On the occasion of the 73rd birthday of the Hon'ble Prime Minister of the country, the Government took a decision vide letter dated 03 October, 2023 to implement the "Namo 11-Point Programme" in the State, incorporating various public welfare initiatives. Accordingly, a proposal to start 73 Divyang Rehabilitation Centres under the "Namo Divyang Shakti Abhiyan" was under consideration of the Government. Regarding this, the Government has taken the following decision:-

Government Resolution :-
Under the Central Government's SIPDA Scheme, District Disability Rehabilitation Centres (DDRCs) in the state provide various services and facilities to persons with disabilities, such as therapy, survey, counseling and assistance for various schemes, generating public awareness, arranging camps for prompt diagnosis and early intervention to provide assistive aids, and providing surgical facilities, various trainings, etc., with the help of government and charitable institutions. A committee under the chairmanship of the concerned District Collector has been constituted to oversee the working of the District Disability Rehabilitation Centres. Honorarium to staff, office expenses, equipment/facility costs, etc. in District Disability Rehabilitation Centres are provided as grants as prescribed by the Central Government. Under this, currently District Rehabilitation Centres are functional in 21 districts.

On the same lines, to make available necessary facilities and help to persons with disabilities in the state under "Namo Divyang Shakti Abhiyan", 72 Divyang Rehabilitation Centres at the rate of two per district and an additional 1 for Mumbai City and Mumbai Suburban, making a total of 73 Divyang Rehabilitation Centres, are to be started under a special campaign. This special campaign shall be named "Namo Divyang Shakti Abhiyan". Through the provision of basic treatment, surgery as per requirement, appliances, and assistive devices to persons with disabilities in the state, support and social security for rehabilitation

to provide, for providing government services related to persons with disabilities, the following amenities and facilities should be made available through the Divyang Rehabilitation Centres under the said campaign :-

  2. To provide disability certificates, transport and railway passes, and other facilities available to persons with disabilities,
  3. To provide necessary equipment/aids to persons with disabilities,
  4. To extend benefits of schemes being implemented for persons with disabilities,
  5. To provide technical and vocational training to persons with disabilities,
  6. To provide capital and loans to persons with disabilities for setting up businesses,
  7. To conduct counseling of persons with disabilities and their parents/guardians.

For providing these services, the 21 District Divyang Rehabilitation Centres currently functional under the SIPDA scheme in the state, as specified in Appendix "A", are hereby designated. The said institutions shall remain responsible for providing services to persons with disabilities in their designated jurisdiction under the SIPDA scheme. Excluding the 21 District Divyang Rehabilitation Centres designated under the Namo Abhiyan, the selection of the remaining Divyang Rehabilitation Centres out of 73 shall be done as follows:-

1. Selection procedure of Institution for District Divyang Rehabilitation Centre:-

The District Social Welfare Officer shall prepare a list of non-governmental organizations registered under the National Trust in their district as well as non-governmental organizations having obtained registration certificates for initiatives under the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016, verify the prescribed criteria for organizations willing to run rehabilitation centres, select them, and submit to the Commissioner, Divyang Welfare, Pune within seven days.

The Commissioner, Divyang Welfare, Pune shall scrutinize the documents of the said organizations and their work done in the field of disability, select two organizations per district including the district centre for each district within 10 days, and submit the list of organizations to the Government. The said organizations will be finalized at the Secretary level of the department.

Also, the supervision and monitoring of District Divyang Rehabilitation Centres started under Namo Divyang Shakti shall remain with the Commissioner, Divyang Welfare, Pune.

While selecting Non-Governmental Organizations (NGOs), the organization must fulfill the following criteria:-

  1. Registration Certificate of the organization under the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016
  2. Experience of implementing various schemes and initiatives for persons with disabilities,
  3. The organization must have its own owned premises, building or an adequate building taken on lease agreement, and the said building must be barrier-free and accessible.
  4. Availability of expert persons for counseling and therapy as well as required and adequate manpower.

  1. Details of the bank account of the existing organization should be available / obtained.

2. Action Plan of work to be undertaken by the Institution after selection for District Divyang Rehabilitation Centre:

  1. Organizing camps for persons with disabilities at the Taluka level in coordination with District and Sub-District Hospitals as per requirement / necessity.
  2. Providing information about various schemes of the Central and State Government for persons with disabilities.
  3. Completing documentation of persons with disabilities for railway/bus passes, submitting it to the concerned administrative system, and following up to obtain the same.
  4. Registering all persons with disabilities in the jurisdiction.
  5. Coordinating with the concerned disabled persons, administrative departments, and health machinery to assist in obtaining U.D.I.D. cards.
  6. Counseling parents of children with disabilities.
  7. Assisting in prompt diagnosis and early intervention/treatment for children with disabilities in the age group of zero to six years.

The said centres shall remain open on all other days of the month except Saturdays and Sundays (minimum 24 days). Also, it will be mandatory to carry out office work for a minimum of 8 hours per day.

3. Manpower and Availability of Funds:

Excluding District Divyang Rehabilitation Centres, other organizations selected for this campaign shall be required to appoint and maintain the following experts and staff :-

  1. Rehabilitation Psychologist
  2. Occupational Therapist / Physiotherapist
  3. Sr. Prosthetist/Orthotist
  4. Multi Purpose Rehabilitation Worker
  5. Attendance cum peon cum Messenger

4. Publicity and Advertisement of the Scheme:

For effective implementation of "Namo Divyang Shakti Abhiyan", the Commissionerate of Divyang Welfare shall take the following action.

  1. Information regarding various facilities provided through District Rehabilitation Centres should reach persons with disabilities; for this purpose, all broadcast media, social media, etc., should be utilized.
  2. A short video regarding information about the campaign of Hon'ble Chief Minister, Hon'ble Deputy Chief Minister, Secretary (Divyang Welfare) and Commissioner, Divyang Welfare should be prepared and published.
  3. A WhatsApp Channel regarding "Namo Divyang Shakti Abhiyan" should be created.

  1. Publicity and advertisement of the campaign should be done on Chief Minister's Office, Deputy Chief Minister's Office, Directorate General of Information and Public Relations, as well as Social Media Handles and websites of all districts.
  2. Also, the Commissionerate of Divyang Welfare and all District Social Welfare Officers, Zilla Parishad shall publish a press note of the "Namo Divyang Shakti Abhiyan" programme.
  3. All District Offices shall send SMS and e-mails to all persons with disabilities in the district through NIC's Bulk SMS and Bulk e-mail services.
  4. Along with this, District Collector, Chief Executive Officer of Zilla Parishad, Civil Surgeon / Chief Medical Officer, and District Health Officer shall give information about the campaign through radio messages.

5. Guidelines for camps to be organized by Non-Governmental Organizations under Namo Divyang Shakti Abhiyan shall be as follows:-

  1. As per necessity, the said 73 Namo Rehabilitation Centres should organize camps in two phases at the Taluka place. The camp should be organized mainly in the premises of the Sub-District Hospital at the Taluka place or at places where transportation and communication facilities are good.
  2. The said camps shall be planned and based on local needs, conditions, and availability of medical and rehabilitation staff. The planning of camps shall be done by the organization in coordination with the concerned Social Welfare Officer, Government Medical Officers, medical staff, other agency officers, and NGOs. More camps should be organized in tribal areas as well as hilly and remote areas compared to others. However, while doing so, care should be taken to ensure that maximum persons with disabilities benefit from the scheme.
  3. Services of rehabilitation professionals working in hospitals and districts, Fitment Centres under Health Department, Sarva Shiksha Abhiyan special schools, organizations running rehabilitation courses, service centres, non-governmental organizations, and other NGO-run schemes and centres shall be utilized for the camp.
  4. Various organizations such as Red Cross, District Funds, Corporate Houses, Rotary, and Lions Club, etc. should be involved in the camp.
  5. To provide all facilities to persons with disabilities, help should be taken from special schools, special teachers, non-governmental organizations, NGO workers, orphanages, residential schools, old age homes, and day care centres in that region.
  6. Adequate publicity through local media should be carried out so that persons with disabilities get information about the camp as well as various facilities provided through District Rehabilitation Centres.
  7. In the first camp, identification process, primary examination, and application process for various schemes will be conducted, and in the second camp, appliances and certificates will be distributed based on the evaluation done for each beneficiary.

  1. Based on the registration done in the camp, a rehabilitation plan will be prepared for each person with disability. For them, required therapy, corrective surgery, assistance and equipment, education, vocational training, employment, and pension support, etc., will be provided.
  2. After the first camp, the second camp should be organized mainly after a gap of fifteen days and with preparation.
  3. Verification of the list of beneficiaries from the first camp should be done, and arrangements for supply of equipment as per requirement should be made in the second camp. Also, if children in schools require corrective surgery, such cases should be referred to District Hospitals.

Also, the Commissionerate should submit a detailed report of the said campaign to the Government every three months.
