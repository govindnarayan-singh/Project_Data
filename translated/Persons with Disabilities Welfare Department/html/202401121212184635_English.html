Preamble :-

Approval was granted as a special case under Read No. 2 to the isolated post of Superintendent (Rector) for the hostel of the Residential School for the Blind, Chikhaldara, run by the National Association for the Welfare of the Handicapped (NAWF), Amravati, so that students with disabilities are not inconvenienced. The proposal to grant post-facto approval to the said post was under consideration of the Government. Accordingly, the Government has taken the following decision.

Government Resolution :-

Before submitting proposals regarding creation of new posts, declaring posts lapsed, reviving lapsed posts, etc. in Government and Semi-Government offices as well as Public Sector Undertakings, Corporations, Aided Institutions, etc. of the Government to the High Level Committee of Secretaries constituted vide Finance Department Government Resolution dated 10th September, 2001, it is necessary to submit the said proposals for approval of the High Level Committee of Secretaries with the recommendation of the Sub-Committee constituted vide Government Resolution dated 09th June, 2017. Accordingly, in the 6th meeting of the year 2023 held on 13th June, 2023 of the said Sub-Committee, recommendation was made to grant approval to the post of Superintendent (Rector) (S-6: 19900-63200) for the hostel of the Residential School for the Blind, Chikhaldara, run by the National Association for the Welfare of the Handicapped (NAWF), Amravati, and submit the proposal for approval of the Hon. High Level Committee of Secretaries. In accordance therewith, pursuant to the discussion in the meeting of the Hon. High Level Committee of Secretaries held on 30.10.2023 and as per the minutes issued on 07.11.2023, post-facto approval has been granted to the said post.
