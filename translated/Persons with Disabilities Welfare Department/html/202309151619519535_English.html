Through the Maharashtra State Divyang Finance and Development Corporation Ltd., Mumbai, various loan schemes are being implemented in the State to provide financial assistance in the form of loans for self-employment to persons with disabilities (Divyang).

2. For the Maharashtra State Divyang Finance and Development Corporation Ltd. for the financial year 2023-24, under Demand No. ZI-4, 4235, Capital Outlay on Social Security and Welfare, 54-Investments, a budgetary provision of Rs. 7 Crore has been made under the object of Share Capital Contribution (Program). Vide Government Resolution referred at Sr. No. 4 of the Department, funds amounting to Rs. 70 Lakhs have been distributed to the Corporation for the financial year 2023-24. Pursuant to the circular of the Finance Department referred at Sr. No. 3, under the object of Share Capital Contribution, 54-Investments, funds within the limit of 10% have been made available by the Finance Department on the Budget Estimation, Allocation and Monitoring System (BEAMS). The matter of distributing the funds made available on the BEAMS system to the Maharashtra State Divyang Finance and Development Corporation Ltd. was under consideration of the Government.
