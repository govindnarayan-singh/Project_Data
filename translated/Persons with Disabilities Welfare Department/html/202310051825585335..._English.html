Preamble :-

For the economic, social, educational, and cultural development of persons with disabilities, along with effective implementation of various schemes undertaken for them, as well as for their all-round development, an independent Department of Disability Welfare has been created vide Government Notification referred to at Sr. No. 1 of the General Administration Department. As per the Government Resolution referred to at Sr. No. 2 dated 24 March, 2023, sanction was granted to continue 353 temporary posts under the Commissioner, Disability Welfare, Pune from 01.03.2023 to 31.08.2023. The matter of granting an extension of tenure from 01.09.2023 to 29.02.2024 to 353 temporary posts on the establishment of the Commissionerate for Persons with Disabilities, Pune during the financial year 2023-24 was under the consideration of the Government.

Government Decision :-

Sanction is hereby granted to continue 353 temporary posts on the establishment of the Commissioner, Disability Welfare, Pune from 01.09.2023 to 29.02.2024, subject to the following terms and conditions :-

  1. Excluding the post of Head of Office, remaining posts vacant for a period of more than six months should be excluded.
  2. Excluding executive posts, this extension of tenure is granted subject to the assumption that other temporary posts have not remained vacant for more than six months.
  3. The temporary posts should be maintained in compliance with the reasons, terms, and conditions subject to which they were sanctioned.
  4. As and from the dates officers / employees on supernumerary posts are absorbed into other posts, such supernumerary posts shall automatically stand cancelled from those respective dates.
