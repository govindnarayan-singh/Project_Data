Preamble :-

In order to enable officers from all departments of the Government and all official machinery at the district level to be present at a single place, listen to the grievances of persons with disabilities (Divyang), and dispose of the work on the spot itself, a decision to implement the campaign "Divyang Welfare Ministry at the Doorsteps of the Divyang" state-wide was taken vide Government Resolution dated 23 May, 2023. Also, to implement the said campaign, a committee was constituted under the chairmanship of Hon'ble Shri Omprakash alias Bachchu Kadu, Member of Legislative Assembly. Similarly, the working procedure regarding this has been finalized vide Circular dated 28 July, 2023. Reconstitution of the committee constituted vide the said Government Resolution dated 23 May, 2023 was under the consideration of the Government. Accordingly, the Government has taken the following decision.

Government Resolution :-

A state-level committee was constituted vide Government Resolution dated 23 May, 2023 regarding policy and implementation in respect of various matters to be undertaken in the campaign "Divyang Welfare Department at the Doorsteps of the Divyang". Reconstitution of the said committee is being done as follows per the directions of the Hon'ble Chief Minister.
