Preamble:-

In the meeting of the Hon'ble Cabinet held on 08 March, 2019, discussions were held regarding various demands of the State Level Deaf Association. Approval was accorded vide the above-cited Government Resolution to appoint a Cabinet Sub-Committee to take decisions regarding various demands of the State Level Deaf Association. The matter of finalizing the Cabinet Sub-Committee pursuant to the said decision was under the consideration of the Government.

Government Resolution:-

Pursuant to the decision taken in the meeting of the Hon'ble Cabinet held on 08 March, 2019, approval is hereby granted by this Government Resolution to appoint the following Cabinet Sub-Committee to take decisions regarding the demands of the State Level Deaf Association:-

  1. Shri Eknath Shinde, Chief Minister and Minister (Persons with Disabilities Welfare Department) - Chairman
  2. Shri Vijaykumar Gavit, Minister for Tribal Development - Member
  3. Shri Deepak Kesarkar, Minister for School Education - Member
  4. Secretary (Persons with Disabilities Welfare) - Member Secretary
