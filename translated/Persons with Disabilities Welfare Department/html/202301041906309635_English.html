Preamble :-

In the meeting of the Hon. Cabinet held on 29th November, 2022, approval was granted to separate the Divyang Kalyan-1 and Divyang Kalyan-2 desks under the Social Justice and Special Assistance Department to create an independent "Divyang Welfare Department", as well as to create a total of 2063 posts of officers / employees at the Mantralaya level independent department, Commissioner, Divyang Welfare, regional level, district level, and taluka level. Pursuant to this, the "Divyang Welfare Department" was created vide Government Notification of the General Administration Department dated 15th December, 2022. Therefore, in accordance with the approval given for creation of posts in the 6th meeting of the year 2022 of the High-Level Committee of Secretaries held on Thursday, 29th December, 2022, the matter of creation of posts for the "Divyang Welfare Department" was under the consideration of the Government.

Government Resolution :-

In accordance with the decision in the meeting of the Hon. Cabinet dated 29th November, 2022 and the approval given by the High-Level Committee of Secretaries, approval is hereby granted for the creation of a total of 32 posts as shown in the statement below for the newly established "Divyang Welfare Department" at the Mantralaya level and for procuring 10 services through outsourcing.

2. While getting work done through outsourcing, the provisions in the Government Resolution of the Finance Department dated 27th April, 2022 should be complied with.

3. The procedure regarding allotment of an independent Budget Head for the newly created Department is underway. As this is expected to take some time, the expenditure to be incurred in the present situation should be met from the sanctioned provision under the budget head Demand No. N-2, 2251 - Secretariat Social Services, (00), 090 Secretariat, (01) Social Justice and Special Assistance Department, (01) (01) Social Justice and Special Assistance Department (Compulsory), (22510113), 01 Salaries.

4. The said Government Resolution will be effective from 30th December, 2022.

5. This Government Resolution is being issued with the approval of the Hon. Cabinet and with the concurrence of the Finance Department vide its Informal Reference No. 09/ Expenditure-14, dated 03rd January, 2023 and Informal Reference No. 01/ A.Pu.Ka., dated 02nd January, 2023.
