Preamble:-

Various loan schemes are being implemented in the State through Maharashtra State Handicapped Finance and Development Corporation Ltd., Mumbai to provide financial assistance in the form of loans to Divyang persons for self-employment.

2. For the Maharashtra State Handicapped Finance and Development Corporation Ltd. for the financial year 2023-24, under Demand No. ZI-3, 2235, Social Security and Welfare (Compulsory) (2235 D364) scheme head, under the item 31-Grant-in-aid (Non-Salary), a budgetary provision of Rs. 90.21 Lakh has been made. As per the Government Resolution referred at Sr. No. 3 of the department, an amount of Rs. 9 Lakh was disbursed to the Corporation for the financial year 2023-24. In accordance with the circular referred at Sr. No. 2 of the Finance Department, funds up to the limit of 10% under the item 31-Grant-in-aid (Non-Salary) have been made available by the Finance Department on the Budget Estimation, Allocation and Monitoring System (BEAMS). The matter of disbursing the funds made available on the BEAMS system to the Maharashtra State Handicapped Finance and Development Corporation was under consideration of the Government.

Government Decision:-

Government sanction is hereby accorded to disburse a total grant of Rs. 9,04,200/- (Rupees Nine Lakh Four Thousand Two Hundred Only) within the limit of 10% of the approved budgetary provision under the item 31-Grant-in-aid (Non-Salary) under the scheme head Demand No. ZI-3, 2235, Social Security and Welfare (Compulsory) (2235 D364) to the Maharashtra State Handicapped Finance and Development Corporation Ltd. for the financial year 2023-24.
