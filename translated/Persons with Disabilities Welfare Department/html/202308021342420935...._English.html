Date: 01 August, 2023.

Government Order:-

Since Shri Omprakash Deshmukh, IAS, retired from service on 31.07.2023 (A.N.) upon reaching the age of superannuation from the post of Commissioner, Persons with Disabilities Welfare, Maharashtra State, the additional charge of the vacant post of Commissioner, Persons with Disabilities Welfare, Maharashtra State, Pune is hereby entrusted to Shri Vishnudas Pundlikrao Ghodke, Deputy Secretary, Persons with Disabilities Welfare Department, Mantralaya until further orders.

2. Shri Ghodke should look after the work of the post of additional charge in addition to handling the duties of his substantive post.

3. This order shall come into force with immediate effect.

4. This Government Order is available on the website of the Government of Maharashtra www.maharashtra.gov.in and its Computer Code Number is 202308021342420935. This order is being issued by certifying with a digital signature.

By order and in the name of the Governor of Maharashtra,
