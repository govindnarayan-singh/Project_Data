1. General Administration Department Notification No. MAHBIL/2009/317415, Dated 15 December, 2022

Office Order:

As per the Notification dated 15 December, 2022 of the General Administration Department, an independent Mantralaya Department named "Persons with Disabilities Welfare" has been created out of the Social Justice and Special Assistance Department. Also, the Hon'ble Cabinet in its meeting dated 29 November, 2022 has given approval for the creation of a total of 2063 posts. For the said department, space is urgently required for a total of 56 posts in Mantralaya. The General Administration Department has informed that space is not available in Mantralaya for the said Persons with Disabilities Welfare Department. Also, as per Serial No. 10, Rule No. 115 in Financial Powers Rules Booklet, 1978 Part-I, Sub-Department-One, full powers have been delegated to the Administrative Department regarding obtaining approval for taking space on rent for the office and paying rent. The General Administration Department has informed to take action for renting space for the office by utilizing the said powers.

2. For the Persons with Disabilities Welfare Department, space of a total of 3080 sq. ft. is required. Accordingly, approval is being granted to take a total carpet area of 2842 sq. ft. on rental basis, comprising 1704 sq. ft. carpet area at 31 A and 32 A, 3rd Floor, A Wing, Mittal Tower from Cyber Windows Pvt. Ltd., and 1138 sq. ft. carpet area at 35 A from Superb System Company Pvt. Ltd. Also, Shri. Vishnudas P. Ghodke, Deputy Secretary is authorized on behalf of the Persons with Disabilities Welfare Department, Mantralaya to sign the said lease agreement.

3. Approval is being granted for payment of monthly rent of Rs. 4,01,717/- to Cyber Windows Pvt. Ltd. for 1704 sq. ft. carpet area at 31 A and 32 A, and monthly rent of Rs. 2,68,283/- to Superb System Company Pvt. Ltd. for 1138 sq. ft. carpet area at 35 A, making a total of Rs. 6,70,000/- (Rupees Six Lakh Seventy Thousand Only), as well as permissible G.S.T. charges.

4. Sanction is being accorded to meet the expenditure incurred towards rent of the said premises from the following head of account.
Demand No. ZI-2,

Major Head - 2251, Secretariat-Social Services
Sub Major Head - 00,
Minor Head - 090, Secretariat
Group Head - (1) Persons with Disabilities Welfare Department
Sub Group Head - (1)(01) Persons with Disabilities Welfare Department (Mandatory) (22511381)
Object Head - 14, Rent, Lease and Taxes

5. This office order is being issued under the powers conferred upon the Administrative Department regarding obtaining sanction for renting premises for office and payment of rent as per Serial No. 10, Rule No. 115 of Financial Powers Rules Booklet, 1978 Part-First, Sub-Department-One. The said Government Order is being passed in pursuance of the sanction given by the General Administration Department for taking premises on rental basis vide Unofficial Reference No. 13/2023/22, dated 22 February, 2023.
