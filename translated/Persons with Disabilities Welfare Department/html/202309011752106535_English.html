Preamble:-
In the matter of Contempt Petition No. 1653/2018 Shri Gaurav Kumar Bansal Vs. Union of India & Ors. filed in the Hon' Supreme Court, the Hon' Supreme Court, vide order dated 06.07.2021, directed the relocation of cured mentally ill persons as per Mental Healthcare Act, 2017. In this matter, in the meeting held on 23.07.2021 by the Committee constituted under the Chairmanship of the Hon' Chief Secretary vide Government Resolution dated 14.10.2019, directions were given to submit a proposal regarding the establishment of rehabilitation homes and to execute the scheme after taking the approval of the Hon' Cabinet. As per the provisions of Section 24 and 25 under Chapter 5 of the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016, it is mentioned that the State Government should establish rehabilitation homes from the perspective of social security and health of persons with disabilities. Also, as per Section 18 under Mental Healthcare Act, 2017, it is stated that it is the responsibility of the appropriate Government, i.e., the Government of Maharashtra, to establish rehabilitation homes in the nature of half-way homes / sheltered accommodation / supported accommodation for cured mentally ill persons who do not require further treatment or cured mentally ill persons who are homeless or cured mentally ill persons whose families do not accept them. Pursuant to these provisions, the matter of framing a scheme for establishing rehabilitation homes through non-governmental organizations for cured mentally ill persons who do not require further treatment or cured mentally ill persons who are homeless or cured mentally ill persons whose families do not accept them was under the consideration of the Government.

Government Resolution No: Divyang-2019/C.R.103/Divyang Kalyan-2.

Government Resolution :-

Government hereby accords sanction to implement the "Scheme of Rehabilitation Homes for Cured Mentally Ill Persons through Non-Governmental Organizations" for cured mentally ill persons who do not require further treatment or cured mentally ill persons who are homeless or cured mentally ill persons whose families do not accept them.

The terms and conditions of the said scheme shall be as follows :-

Nature of the Scheme :-

  1. Number of Rehabilitation Homes :- 16 (Minimum 25 intake capacity)
  2. Location of Rehabilitation Homes :- The said rehabilitation homes shall be located near the Government Regional Mental Hospitals currently run by the State Government at Thane, Pune, Nagpur, and Ratnagiri.
  3. Admission Criteria for Rehabilitation Homes :-
    1. For admission to the said rehabilitation home, the management of any rehabilitation home shall not discriminate on the basis of caste, religion, race, etc.
    2. Patients who recover from mental illness as per Section 85 to 99 under Mental Healthcare Act, 2017 from Government or Semi-Government Mental Hospitals and such cured mentally ill persons who do not require further treatment or cured mentally ill persons who are homeless, or cured mentally ill persons whose families or relatives accepting them would be the best option, but if their families do not accept them, then for such persons, based on the certificate issued by the Medical Officer of the concerned Regional Mental Hospital certifying that they are eligible for rehabilitation, the Head of the concerned Rehabilitation Home shall grant admission to the concerned cured mentally ill person in the said rehabilitation home.
    3. Admission process in the said rehabilitation home shall remain limited to the proportion of available seats. If seats are not available in the concerned rehabilitation home, admission shall be given in the rehabilitation home of the nearest division.
    4. However, under any circumstances, no cured mentally ill person shall be given direct admission into such rehabilitation home through a private mechanism.
  4. Criteria for Discharge/Release from Rehabilitation Home :-
    1. The primary responsibility of the said rehabilitation home shall be to rehabilitate the cured mentally ill person admitted in the said rehabilitation home into his / her family. Under this, the mechanism of the rehabilitation home, with the assistance of the concerned Regional Mental Hospital and police mechanism, shall track down the family or relatives of the concerned cured mentally ill person who can take custody and look after him / her, and take action to hand over the concerned cured mentally ill person to the concerned family or relatives.
    2. In respect of inmates for whom action could not be taken as per paragraph A above, who are to be discharged from the rehabilitation home for rehabilitation in an environment outside the rehabilitation home,

is to be done, regarding the period spent by that inmate in the rehabilitation home, treatment, therapy, training received during that period, whether the inmate has acquired the capacity to stand on his/her own feet and earn a livelihood after release in society, behavior of the inmate regarding the staff in the rehabilitation home, etc., taking into consideration various records in the rehabilitation home as well as based on the joint and self-explicit opinion of Psychiatrist, Occupational Therapist, and Social Worker, the Head of the Rehabilitation Home shall issue orders releasing the concerned inmate from the rehabilitation home.

  1. Responsibilities of Management of Concerned Rehabilitation Homes After Release :-
    1. After the concerned person is released under the above process from the rehabilitation home, the responsibility of comprehensive follow-up and action regarding other incidental arrangements of him/her shall lie with the concerned rehabilitation home.
    2. After release, the inmate shall spend his/her life under the supervision of the staff of the concerned rehabilitation home, and the Social Worker shall submit an inquiry report to the rehabilitation home every 03 months or whenever necessary regarding the behavior of the concerned inmate in society, in the family, where he/she works, and to the colleagues in the office.
    3. If under the said inquiry report it comes to notice that the concerned inmate requires treatment, the Head of the concerned rehabilitation home shall apply to the Medical Officer of the adjacent Regional Mental Hospital for re-admission of the concerned inmate, submit the necessary report, and take action to admit the concerned inmate to the Regional Mental Hospital for treatment. After the concerned inmate recovers and becomes eligible for rehabilitation, the concerned Medical Officer shall also take action to re-admit him/her to the concerned rehabilitation home.
    4. In case an inmate in a rehabilitation home or an inmate who has been rehabilitated outside the rehabilitation home meets with an accident or suffers serious injury due to any other reason, or if the person goes missing or dies, the concerned Head of the Home shall take necessary measures within 24 hours and inform all concerned authorities about the same in writing.
  2. Facilities, Training, etc. to be provided to Inmates by Rehabilitation Home :-
    1. Training :- Assessing the interest and inclination of the concerned inmate, emphasizing the development of personal skills, conducting continuous activity programs (Engaging Activity) for inmates, as well as conducting skill-based development programs and training for inmates, so that the inmate released from the rehabilitation home gets a means of livelihood.
    2. Rehabilitation :- Planning rehabilitative programs to bring potential released inmates into the main stream of society as well as to make them self-reliant, and taking measures. This will include socialization of the concerned inmate, agricultural training, household maintenance, cleanliness, training appropriate to the times such as Data Entry Operator.

  1. Counseling :- Conducting individual counseling as well as group counseling of all inmates in the rehabilitation home through counseling methods, and making efforts for training and rehabilitation according to the progress of each inmate.
  2. Medical Treatment :- For inmates admitted in mental hospitals, continuing the ongoing treatment method in the rehabilitation home as per requirement, and keeping all types of medicines available as per the requirement of the inmate. Using various therapies, etc.
  3. Accommodation :- Making independent accommodation arrangements for boys, girls, women, men, elderly women, and elderly men, arranging independent accommodation with a minimum of two or maximum of four beds in a minimum of one room, providing all amenities/facilities in accommodation rooms as per inmates, making independent arrangements for bathrooms and toilets.
  4. Food :- It shall be mandatory for the management to provide nutritious food to inmates in the rehabilitation home. This will include morning tea/milk and breakfast, afternoon lunch, evening snacks, and night dinner. As per the demand of inmates, vegetarian (which includes sweet dishes) and non-vegetarian food once a week must be provided. Keeping dining hall and kitchen independent, providing necessary food utensils and facilities according to the number of inmates in both homes, e.g. plate, bowl, glass, cooking utensils, gas, food grain container, etc.
  5. Sports and Entertainment :- Implementing mental development programs through recreational facilities, various sports equipment, yoga, vipassana in the rehabilitation home for inmates.
  6. Cleanliness, Nursing and Care :- Taking necessary care of adolescent girls and women during their menstrual cycle. Also maintaining cleanliness of rehabilitation center, kitchen room, dining hall, place of residence, toilets, etc. Apart from this, taking necessary nursing and care of sick inmates through caregivers.
  7. Protection :- Keeping the premises of the rehabilitation home properly protected with wire fencing or RCC wall. Taking precaution that no injury or harm is caused to co-inmates or staff in the rehabilitation home by inmates in the rehabilitation center.
  8. Establishing Inter-connection and Coordination with Government and Semi-Government Departments :- To get the benefit of necessary amenities/facilities under the training and rehabilitation program determined for release from the rehabilitation home, the management of the rehabilitation home shall maintain contact and coordination with nearby various Government and Semi-Government offices. In this manner, the liability of the rehabilitation home towards the inmates shall remain.

Clarification regarding Cured Mentally Ill Children below 18 years of age :-
As per Section 18, Sub-section 4, Clause (e) under Mental Healthcare Act, 2017, there is a provision to provide necessary services regarding cured mentally ill children. Accordingly, regarding this scheme, it is hereby clarified that in respect of cured mentally ill children below 18 years of age, as per the provisions under the Juvenile Justice Act, 2015, action through the Child Welfare Committee shall be necessary regarding placing them in Government institutions under the jurisdiction of the Women and Child Development Department of the State Government.

8. Number of Rehabilitation Homes.

In the first phase, a total of 16 Rehabilitation Homes are being established at Nagpur, Pune, Thane, and Ratnagiri, with four homes each for Males (Adult & Senior) and Females (Adult & Senior). In subsequent phases, rehabilitation homes will be established according to necessity and requirement. Taking into consideration the increasing number of cured mentally ill persons as per the recommendation of the concerned Regional Mental Hospital, the number of rehabilitation homes and the number of persons may be increased from time to time through the Department as per requirement.

9. Geographical Limits of Rehabilitation Homes :-

It shall remain within the district boundaries of the headquarters of Regional Mental Hospitals in the cities of Nagpur, Pune, Thane, and Ratnagiri.

10. Grant-in-aid sanctioned per beneficiary / inmate to Non-Governmental Organization :-

For Non-Governmental Organizations running rehabilitation homes, financial grant-in-aid of approximately Rs. 12,000/- per person per month shall be sanctioned for items such as staff honorarium, food, personal amenities, medical and personal hygiene expenses, recreational facilities, contingency expenses, furniture per inmate, utensils, bed, bedding - blankets, cooking utensils, other useful material, incidental services, as well as building rent, etc. in the rehabilitation home.

11. Beneficiaries of the Scheme :-

Cured mentally ill persons above 18 years of age certified for rehabilitation by the Medical Superintendent of the concerned Regional Mental Hospital, who do not require further treatment or who are homeless or whose family or relatives do not accept them.

12. Administrative Department Controlling the Scheme :-

The said scheme shall remain functional under the control of the Persons with Disabilities Welfare Department.

13. Supervision and Monitoring Officers of the Scheme :-

The Commissioner, Persons with Disabilities Welfare, Maharashtra State, Pune and the Director, Health Services & National Health Mission, Mumbai shall jointly remain the supervising and monitoring officers.

14. Contact and Control Officers :-

The concerned District Social Welfare Officer, Zilla Parishad and the concerned Medical Superintendent, Regional Mental Hospital shall jointly remain contact and control officers.

15. Implementation Agency of the Scheme :-

Non-Governmental Organizations possessing a valid registration certificate to run a rehabilitation home registered as a Public Company under the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016, Mental Healthcare Act, 2017, Societies Registration Act, 1860, Maharashtra Public Trusts Act, 1950, or Companies Act, 2013 shall be the implementing agency for this scheme.

16. Procedure for Selection of Eligible Organizations :-

  1. For selecting eligible organizations for implementation of the said scheme, a State-Level Selection Committee shall be as follows:
    1. Additional Chief Secretary / Principal Secretary / Secretary, Persons with Disabilities Welfare Department - Ex-Officio Chairman
    2. Commissioner, Persons with Disabilities Welfare Commissionerate, Maharashtra State, Pune - Member
    3. Commissioner, Health Services and Director, National Health Mission, Mumbai - Member
    4. Joint Secretary / Deputy Secretary, Finance Department - Member
    5. Joint Secretary / Deputy Secretary, Public Health Department - Member
    6. Joint Secretary / Deputy Secretary, Persons with Disabilities Welfare Department - Member Secretary
  2. Proposals from eligible organizations shall be invited by the Persons with Disabilities Welfare Department of the Government by issuing advertisements through public notice media / releasing public notices on the official website of the Government.
    1. Scrutiny and verification of received proposals shall be carried out by the Committee.
    2. Accordingly, selection of eligible organizations shall be done by the said Committee as per merit/marking, and accordingly, the decision of the said Committee shall be final.
    3. After selection of eligible organizations, Government Resolution shall be issued by the Department.
    4. It shall be binding on the concerned organization to start the said initiative within the prescribed time limit mentioned in the Government Resolution. If the said initiative is not started within the prescribed time limit, the selection of the said organization shall be cancelled by the Department without any prior notice. Thereafter, action shall be taken from the Government level to give opportunity to run the concerned rehabilitation home to the organization standing first, second, third in order of merit in the waiting list.
    5. After selection of the organization, a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) shall be executed between the organization and the Government regarding the operation of the rehabilitation home.
    6. The validity period of the said waiting list shall not exceed one year under any circumstances.

17. Criteria for Selection of Organizations :-

A) The following terms and conditions shall apply to non-governmental organizations desirous of running the Rehabilitation Home Scheme for cured mentally ill persons through non-governmental organizations:

  1. It is mandatory for the organization to possess a registration certificate as a registered Non-Governmental Organization running a rehabilitation home as a Public Company under the Societies Registration Act, 1860, Maharashtra Public Trusts Act, 1950, or Companies Act, 2013.
  2. It is mandatory for the organization desirous of running a rehabilitation home to possess a valid registration certificate issued by a competent authority as per provisions under the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016 and Mental Healthcare Act, 2017.
  3. The organization implementing the said scheme should not be working for the personal gain of any individual/organization.
  4. It is mandatory for the concerned organization to have at least three years of working experience in the field of mental health.
  5. It is mandatory that the Income Tax Returns for the last three years of the organization and the audit report are certified by a competent and authorized Chartered Accountant.
  6. The financial position of the organization should be good. If for any reason there is a delay in receiving Government grant, the organization should be financially competent to bear the expenses of the rehabilitation home. For this purpose, it is mandatory to have a Bank Guarantee of at least Rs. Ten Lakhs in the name of the organization.
  7. It is mandatory that the area of operation of the organization desirous of implementing the said scheme is Maharashtra State.
  8. It is mandatory for the organization to be registered on the NITI Aayog Portal.
  9. The organization granted approval to run a rehabilitation home under this scheme must obtain a valid registration certificate from a competent authority under the Food and Drug Administration Department of the Government within 6 months from the date of the approval order. If the certificate is not obtained within the prescribed time limit, the approval granted for the rehabilitation home to the concerned organization shall be deemed cancelled without giving any prior notice, and action shall be taken to grant the same to the senior-most organization in order of merit in the waiting list. Thereafter, no claim of the original organization shall be considered.

18. Criteria for Granting Benefits Under the Scheme :-

As per the guidelines dated 18.08.2017 bearing No. 29-01/2017-DD.III of the Central Government, cured mentally ill persons aged 18 years and above who have undergone treatment in Regional Mental Hospitals shall be granted admission to rehabilitation homes. However, if no other alternative is available for such persons, their stay in the rehabilitation home shall be kept permanent.

19. Process for Submitting Proposals for Rehabilitation Home Scheme :-

  1. Public notices inviting proposals from eligible and desirous organizations for setting up rehabilitation homes in Nagpur, Pune, Thane, and Ratnagiri districts should be issued by the Persons with Disabilities Welfare Commissionerate.
  2. Desirous organizations should submit complete proposals to the concerned District Social Welfare Officer, Zilla Parishad within the prescribed time limit.
  3. In connection with the proposals submitted by the said desirous organizations, both officers, i.e., the District Social Welfare Officer, Zilla Parishad and the Medical Superintendent, Regional Mental Hospital, shall jointly inspect the proposals and submit the inspection report along with evaluation and grading to the Commissioner, Persons with Disabilities Welfare, Maharashtra State, Pune.
  4. The Commissioner, Persons with Disabilities Welfare, Maharashtra State, Pune shall verify that the received proposals are complete as per the terms and conditions in the guidelines of the Central Government, satisfy themselves by visiting if required, and submit the proposal to the Government with explicit recommendations.
  5. The proposals received by the Government from the Persons with Disabilities Welfare Commissionerate shall be analyzed by the Selection Committee constituted under the Chairmanship of Additional Chief Secretary / Principal Secretary / Secretary, Persons with Disabilities Welfare Department, based on the evaluation and grading in the inspection report submitted by the inspection officers, and eligible non-governmental organizations shall be selected.
  6. The names of organizations selected by the Selection Committee shall be made available on the Government website through prescribed Government Resolutions issued by the Government.

20. Disbursement Mechanism and Head of Account for Funds :-

Drawing and Disbursing Officer: To implement this scheme and draw and disburse funds, the District Social Welfare Officers of Zilla Parishads of the concerned districts are declared as Drawing and Disbursing Officers. Necessary grants for the said rehabilitation homes shall be sanctioned by the Government. Taking into consideration the admissibility of grant for the concerned rehabilitation home under this scheme, the admissible grant for every 3 months shall be disbursed once every 3 months into the bank account of the concerned NGO through DBT system.

21. Head of Account of the Scheme :-

Until a separate Head of Account is made available by the Government for the implementation of this scheme, grants shall be made available by the Government under the currently existing Head of Account for the Intellectually Disabled category - (08) (05) Assistance to Non-Government Institutions working for the Mentally Ill (Scheme) Head of Account 2235D301, 31- Grant-in-Aid (Non-Salary).

The Commissioner, Persons with Disabilities Welfare, Maharashtra State, Pune is declared as the Drawing and Disbursing Officer in this matter, and he/she or an officer authorized by him/her is authorized to sign the bills.
