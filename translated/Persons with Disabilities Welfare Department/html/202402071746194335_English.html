Preamble:-

Through the Maharashtra State Handicapped Finance and Development Corporation Ltd., Mumbai, various schemes are being implemented for persons with disabilities in the state, such as the National Corporation's Divyang Swavalamban Yojana, Individual Direct Loan Scheme, and eco-friendly mobile shops running on green energy (Mobile Shop on Vehicle) to enable persons with disabilities to become self-reliant.

2. For the Maharashtra State Handicapped Finance and Development Corporation Ltd., for the financial year 2023-24, under Demand No. ZI-3, 2235 Social Security and Welfare, 02 Social Welfare, 101 Welfare of Handicapped, (05) Other Schemes for Disabled, (05) (17) Providing eco-friendly vehicles to persons with disabilities to make them self-reliant (Mobile shop on vehicle) (General) (Program) (2235 D257) 31, Grants-in-aid (Non-Salary), a budget provision of Rs. 15 Crore has been made. As per the Government Resolution cited under reference No. 3 of the Department, funds amounting to Rs. 3 Crore have been released to the Corporation for the financial year 2023-24. In accordance with the Circular under reference No. 2 of the Finance Department, funds for the purpose of Mobile Shop on Vehicle (2235 D257) have been made available by the Finance Department on the Budget Estimation, Allocation and Monitoring System (BEAMS). The matter of releasing the funds available on the BEAMS system to the Maharashtra State Handicapped Finance and Development Corporation Ltd. was under the consideration of the Government.

Government Resolution:-

Government sanction is hereby accorded to release a grant of Rs. 2,00,00,000/- (Rupees Two Crore only) out of the approved budgetary provision for the financial year 2023-24 to the Maharashtra State Handicapped Finance and Development Corporation Ltd. under Demand No. ZI-3, 2235 Social Security and Welfare, Providing eco-friendly vehicles to persons with disabilities to make them self-reliant (Mobile shop on vehicle) (General) (Program) (2235 D257) 31, Grants-in-aid (Non-Salary).
