Preamble:-

Through the Maharashtra State Handicapped Finance and Development Corporation Ltd., Mumbai, various schemes are being implemented in the state such as National Corporation's Divyang Self-Reliance Scheme, Individual Direct Loan Scheme, Environment-friendly Mobile Shop on Vehicle running on green energy to make persons with disabilities self-reliant, etc.

2. For the Maharashtra State Handicapped Finance and Development Corporation Ltd. for the financial year 2023-24, under Demand No. ZI-3, 2235 Social Security and Welfare, 02 Social Welfare, 101 Welfare of Handicapped, (05) Other Schemes for Divyang, (05) (17) Providing environment-friendly vehicles to make persons with disabilities self-reliant (Mobile Shop on Vehicle) (General) (Programme) (2235 D257) 31, Grants-in-Aid (Non-Salary), a budgetary provision of Rs. 15 Crore has been made. Under the Government Resolutions at Reference Nos. 2 and 3 of the Department, a total fund of Rs. 5 Crore has been distributed for the scheme for the financial year 2023-24 under the account head. As per the Circular of the Finance Department at Reference No. 6, out of the funds made available on the BEAMS system within the limit of revised budgetary estimates fixed for mandatory provisions in the current financial year, the matter of releasing funds of Rs. 10 Crore to Maharashtra State Handicapped Finance and Development Corporation Ltd. was under consideration of the Government.

Government Decision:-

Government sanction is hereby accorded to release funds amounting to Rs. 10,00,00,000/- (Rupees Ten Crore only) made available on the BEAMS system within the limit of revised budget estimates under Demand No. ZI-3, 2235 Social Security and Welfare, Providing environment-friendly vehicles to make persons with disabilities self-reliant (Mobile Shop on Vehicle) (General) (Programme) (2235 D257) 31, Grants-in-Aid (Non-Salary) for the financial year 2023-24 to Maharashtra State Handicapped Finance and Development Corporation Ltd.
