Government Circular :-

In accordance with Section-33 of the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016, the Central Government, vide Notification dated 4 January, 2021, prepared a list of posts identified for persons with disabilities, and accordingly, instructions were issued vide Government Resolution of the Social Justice and Special Assistance Department dated 2 February, 2021 regarding the identification of posts on the establishment of the State Government for persons with disabilities.

2. Vide Notification dated 24 August, 2022 of the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment, Government of India, Spinal Deformity (SD) and Spinal Injury (SI) without any associated neurological / limb dysfunction has been included as an independent sub-category under the Locomotor Disability category mentioned in the Central Government's Notification dated 4 January, 2021. Accordingly, all such posts in government service which have been identified for the disability categories OA, OL, BL, OAL, BLOA and BLA under the Locomotor Disability category shall also stand identified for the independent sub-categories Spinal Deformity (SD) and Spinal Injury (SI) without any associated neurological / limb dysfunction.
