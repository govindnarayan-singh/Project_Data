Preamble:-

Various loan schemes are implemented through Maharashtra State Handicapped Finance and Development Corporation Ltd., Mumbai to provide financial assistance in the form of loans for self-employment to persons with disabilities in the State.

2. For the financial year 2023-24, under Demand No. ZI-3, 2235, Social Security and Welfare (Compulsory) (2235 D364), under the scheme head 31-Grants-in-aid (Non-salary), a budgetary provision of Rs. 90.21 Lakhs was made for this purpose. Pursuant to the Government Resolutions at Reference Nos. 3 and 4 of the Department, a total fund of Rs. 18.04 Lakhs was distributed to the Corporation. As per the Circular at Reference No. 6 of the Finance Department, the matter of releasing funds available on the BEAMS system within the limits of the revised budget estimates determined for compulsory provision in the current financial year to the Maharashtra State Handicapped Finance and Development Corporation was under consideration of the Government.

Government Resolution:-

Sanction of the Government is hereby accorded to release grant of total Rs. 72,16,800/- (Rupees Seventy Two Lakh Sixteen Thousand Eight Hundred Only) to Maharashtra State Handicapped Finance and Development Corporation within the limits of the sanctioned revised budget estimates for the financial year 2023-24 under Demand No. ZI-3, 2235, Social Security and Welfare (Compulsory) (2235 D364) scheme head under 31-Grants-in-aid (Non-salary).
