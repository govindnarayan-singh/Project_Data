Preamble :-

For the financial year 2023-24, as per the circular referred to at Sr. No. 1 of the Finance Department, under Demand No. ZI-3, 2235, Social Security and Welfare under the sub-ordinate Maharashtra State Handicapped Finance and Development Corporation Ltd., Mumbai, funds within the limit of 30% for the objective 36-Grants-in-aid (Salary) under mandatory scheme have been released on the Budget Estimation, Allocation and Monitoring System (BEAMS).

Under the Government Resolutions referred to at Sr. No. 3 and 4, a total fund of Rs. 45.90 Lakh has been released to the Corporation. The Managing Director, Maharashtra State Handicapped Finance and Development Corporation Ltd., Mumbai, vide letter referred to at Sr. No. 5 above, submitted a proposal to the Government for release of funds from the sanctioned budgetary provision under the item 36-Grants-in-aid (Salary) for mandatory schemes of the Corporation. Accordingly, the matter of releasing funds was under the consideration of the Government.

Government Resolution :-

Financial assistance in the form of loans is provided to persons with disabilities in the state for self-employment through Maharashtra State Handicapped Finance and Development Corporation Ltd., Mumbai. Sanction is hereby accorded by the Government to release a grant of total Rs. 30,60,480/- (Rupees Thirty Lakh Sixty Thousand Four Hundred and Eighty Only) within the limit of 30% of the budget provision under the item 36-Grants-in-aid (Salary) under scheme head of account Demand No. ZI-3, 2235, Social Security and Welfare (Mandatory) (2235 D364) for this Corporation for the financial year 2023-24. 2. The grant in this Government Resolution is being released subject to the following terms and conditions:

  1. 1) Before drawing the funds available on the Budget Distribution System from the treasury, the guidelines mentioned in paragraphs 4 to 18 and the guidelines in the appendix of the Finance Department's circular dated 12.04.2023 should be strictly implemented.
  2. 2) The Corporation should spend the funds released scheme/scheme-wise and item-wise only on admissible items under the concerned mandatory scheme.
  3. 3) Systematization should be brought into operations with a view to keeping administrative expenditure within limits.
  4. 4) Item-wise detailed information of the amounts spent on various items, expenditure incurred thereon, as well as information regarding unspent funds of the previous year should be submitted to the Government immediately.
  5. 5) The Corporation should make utmost efforts to effectively implement the rate of recovery of loans distributed.
  6. 6) Expenditure should not be incurred merely because there is a budget provision or grant has been released. Economy should be observed in office expenditure.

3. Regarding this administrative grant, the Deputy Secretary (Disability Welfare-2), Persons with Disabilities Welfare Department, Mantralaya, Mumbai shall be the Controlling Officer. The amount of the said grant is being placed at the disposal of the Regional Deputy Commissioner, Social Welfare, Navi Mumbai for further action. As per Finance Department, G.R. No. Mambhin-2022/C.R.24/2022/Regulation, Dated 12.01.2023, the said grant is unconditional. They are authorized to disburse the said grant to Maharashtra State Handicapped Finance and Development Corporation as per the bill format in M.T.C. Rule 44(A).
