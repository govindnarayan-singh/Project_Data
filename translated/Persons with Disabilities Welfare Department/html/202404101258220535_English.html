Preamble:

Pursuant to the General Administration Department Notification under Reference No. 1 dated 15 December, 2022, a new department named Persons with Disabilities Welfare Department has been created out of the Social Justice and Special Assistance Department. Also, posts have been created for this department vide Government Resolution dated 4 January, 2023 of the Persons with Disabilities Welfare Department.

With a view to starting the functioning of the Cash Branch in the department, Shri Sai-Prasad D. Mukdadwar (Section Officer / Di.Ka.-2) was declared as the "Drawing and Disbursing Officer" vide the Government Resolution at Reference No. 3 above.

Now, vide the Finance Department's order at Reference No. 4 above, Shri Mahendra Shankar Achare, Accounts Officer, Nanaji Deshmukh Krushi Sanjeevani Project Management Cell, Jalna, has been posted by transfer to the post of Accounts Officer in the Persons with Disabilities Welfare Department; therefore, the matter of making a change in the Government Resolution dated 24 March, 2023 declaring the "Drawing and Disbursing Officer" was under consideration of the Department.

Government Resolution:-

In connection with the functioning of the Cash Branch in the Persons with Disabilities Welfare Department, pursuant to the provisions in Rule 15 (3) of Volume I of the Maharashtra Treasury Rules, 1968, Shri Sai-Prasad Mukdadwar, Desk Officer (Di.Ka.-2) was declared as the Drawing and Disbursing Officer of the department vide the order cited at Reference No. 3 above.
