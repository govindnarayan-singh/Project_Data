Government Circular :-

In order to solve the difficulties of persons with disabilities (Divyang) of various categories, officers of all departments of the Government and all government machinery can remain present at one place at the district level to understand the grievances of persons with disabilities and redress the work on the spot itself. For this purpose, as per the Government Resolution of this Department dated 23 May 2023, the campaign "Disability Welfare Department at the Doorstep of Persons with Disabilities" is being implemented, and vide the Government Circular of the Department dated 28 July, 2023, the campaign at the district level
