Preamble:-

Pursuant to the above-mentioned reference No. 1 of the Social Justice and Special Assistance Department, for the social and economic development of persons with disabilities, the Maharashtra State Divyang Finance and Development Corporation was established on 27th March, 2002, as a registered institution under Section 25 of the Companies Act, 1956, as a Government undertaking. While establishing the Corporation, the Board of Directors was also determined, and as per the Government Resolution dated 12th September, 2002 under reference No. 2, posts were created for the Maharashtra State Divyang Finance and Development Corporation.

2. The work of providing financial assistance in the form of loans for self-employment to the disabled in the State is being carried out through this Corporation. Along with loan distribution, various schemes like the State Government's direct loan scheme, eco-friendly mobile vehicles running on green energy, Mahasharad web-based portal public awareness campaign, National Divyang Corporation's Divyang Swavalamban Yojana, etc., are being implemented through this Corporation for the overall development of the disabled.

3. In the Cabinet meeting held on 29th November, 2022, approval was given to create an independent "Divyang Kalyan Department" by separating the Divyang Kalyan-1 and Divyang Kalyan-2 desks from the Social Justice and Special Assistance Department. Accordingly, a new ministerial administrative department "Divyang Kalyan Department" has been created vide the Government Notification under reference No. 2 above. ...Divyang Finance and Development Corporation's work was functioning under the Social Justice and Special Assistance Department. Now, as the Divyang Kalyan Department has become independent, the matter of making changes in the Board of Directors of the Maharashtra State Divyang Finance and Development Corporation was under the consideration of the Government.

Government Resolution:-

In accordance with the decision dated 21st November, 2001 of the Social Justice and Special Assistance Department, changes are being made in the structure of the Board of Directors of the Maharashtra State Divyang Finance and Development Corporation appointed, and the revised structure of the Board of Directors will be as follows:-

Sr. No. Office Bearer Designation
1. Hon. Minister, Divyang Kalyan President Ex-officio
2. Hon. State Minister, Divyang Kalyan Vice-President Ex-officio
3. Secretary, Divyang Kalyan Department Member Ex-officio
4. Managing Director, National Handicapped Finance and Development Corporation or their representative Member Ex-officio
5. Commissioner (Equal Opportunities for Persons with Disabilities Act) Member Ex-officio
6. Member from Blind Category Non-official Member Appointment by the Government from those associated with organizations in the field of disability
7. Member from Deaf and Dumb Category Non-official Member
8. Member from Orthopedically Handicapped Category Non-official Member
9. Member from Intellectually Disabled Category Non-official Member
10. Managing Director of the Corporation Member Secretary Ex-officio
