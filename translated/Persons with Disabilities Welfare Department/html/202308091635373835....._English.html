Government Circular :-

To resolve the difficulties of persons with disabilities belonging to various categories at the district level, officers of all government departments and the entire government machinery will remain present at one place, hear the grievances of persons with disabilities, and dispose of the work on the spot itself. For this purpose, as per the Government Resolution dated 23 May 2023 of this Department, "Divyang Kalyan Vibhag Divyanganche Dari" (Persons with Disabilities Welfare Department at the Doorsteps of Persons with Disabilities) campaign is being implemented, and as per the Government Circular dated 28 July 2023 of this Department, the working procedure to be adopted while implementing the said campaign at the district level has been detailed. Along with the said circular, the schedule of the campaign to be organized in all districts has been provided in "Form B".

2. In this context, in the meeting organized on dates 2 and 4 August 2023, the District Social Welfare Officers of Amravati, Nagpur, Gadchiroli, Bhandara, Gondia, Thane, as well as Jalna requested to make changes in the dates of the campaign fixed for their respective districts.
