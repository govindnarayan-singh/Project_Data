Preamble :-

Through the Maharashtra State Handicapped Finance and Development Corporation Ltd., Mumbai, various schemes are being implemented for persons with disabilities in the State, such as the National Corporation's Disability Self-Reliance Scheme, Individual Direct Loan Scheme, Environment-friendly Mobile Shops (Mobile Shop on Vehicle) running on green energy to make persons with disabilities self-reliant, etc.

2. For the financial year 2023-24, a budgetary provision of Rs. 15 Crore was made for Maharashtra State Handicapped Finance and Development Corporation Ltd., Mumbai under Demand No. ZI-3, 2235 Social Security and Welfare, 02 Social Welfare, 101 Welfare of Handicapped, (05) Other Schemes for Persons with Disabilities, (05) (17) Providing environment-friendly vehicles to make persons with disabilities self-reliant (Mobile Shop on Vehicle) (General) (Programme) (2235 D257) 31, Grants-in-aid (Non-Salary). Under the Government Resolutions referred to at Sr. No. 2 and 3 of the Department, a total fund of Rs. 5 Crore has been distributed to the Corporation under this accounting head for the financial year 2023-24. Since the funds available under the accounting head 2235 D364, 31, Grants-in-aid (Non-Salary) were insufficient to provide e-vehicles under the scheme to the maximum eligible persons with disabilities identified under the scheme, a supplementary demand was submitted to the Finance Department. Accordingly, as it was possible to meet this expenditure from the sanctioned grant of the current financial year, a token supplementary demand of Rs. 1,000/- was approved in the Second (Budgetary) Session of the Legislature in February, 2024 under Item No. 141, and a fund of Rs. 5 Crore was sanctioned through the supplementary demand. Out of this, the issue of distributing funds of Rs. 4.90 Crore made available by the Finance Department through the BEAMS system to the Maharashtra State Handicapped Finance and Development Corporation Ltd. was under consideration of the Government.

Government Resolution :-

For the financial year 2023-24, to Maharashtra State Handicapped Finance and Development Corporation Ltd., under Demand No. ZI-3, 2235 Social Security and Welfare, providing environment-friendly vehicles to make persons with disabilities self-reliant (Mobile Shop on Vehicle) (General) (Programme) (2235 D257) 31, Grants-in-aid

(Non-Salary), as the funds available under the accounting head for providing e-vehicles to maximum eligible persons with disabilities under this scheme were insufficient, sanction is hereby accorded by the Government to distribute a fund of Rs. 4,90,00,000/- (Rupees Four Crore Ninety Lakh Only) approved under Item No. 141 in the Second (Budget) Session of the Legislature in February, 2024 to the Maharashtra State Handicapped Finance and Development Corporation Ltd. under this Government Resolution.

2. Before drawing the funds available on the Budget Estimation and Allocations Monitoring System (BEAMS) from the treasury, the guidelines specified in the circulars of the Finance Department dated 12.04.2023 and date 04.03.2024 and the guidelines in the appendix should be strictly implemented.
