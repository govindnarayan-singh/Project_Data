Preamble:-
The registration of Maharashtra State Divyang Finance and Development Corporation has been done under Section-25 of the Companies Act, 1956. At the time of establishment of the Corporation, the authorized share capital of the Corporation was approved as Rupees 50 Crores. Loans are provided to Divyang persons according to the schemes implemented through the Corporation out of the share capital made available to the Corporation from time to time. Also, this Corporation functions as the State Channelising Agency of the National Handicapped Finance and Development Corporation, New Delhi.

2. Pursuant to Social Justice and Special Assistance Department Government Resolution dated 08.06.2022, the authorized share capital of Maharashtra State Divyang Finance and Development Corporation has been increased from Rupees 50 Crores to Rupees 500 Crores. On the Ministry of Corporate Affairs website, for official...

...updating the share capital information, registration fees will have to be paid to the Central Government as per Section SH.7 (Changes in Authorized Capital). Correspondingly, as the authorized share capital of the Corporation has been increased from Rupees 50 Crores to Rupees 500 Crores, the Corporation will have to pay a total fee of Rupees 2,51,19,000/-. Therefore, to make funds available to the Corporation, a supplementary demand for an amount of Rupees 2,51,19,000/- was presented and approved under Item No. 295 in the Second (Monsoon) Session of the Legislature. The matter of disbursing the funds approved through this supplementary demand to the Maharashtra State Divyang Finance and Development Corporation is under consideration of the Government.

Government Resolution:-
Since the authorized share capital of Maharashtra State Divyang Finance and Development Corporation has been increased from Rupees 50 Crores to Rupees 500 Crores, the registration fee required to be paid on the website of Ministry of Corporate Affairs / Registrar of Companies, Mumbai is to be paid from budget head No. 2235 D364, 31, Grants-in-Aid (Non-Salary) in the financial year 2023-24, and a budgetary provision of Rupees 90.21 Lakhs was made under the said budget head in the financial year 2023-24. As the funds under the accounting head of the said scheme were insufficient to disburse the registration fee required to be paid on the website of Ministry of Corporate Affairs / Registrar of Companies, Mumbai for recording the increase in share capital of the Corporation, funds amounting to Rupees 2,51,19,000/- (Rupees Two Crore Fifty One Lakh Nineteen Thousand Only) approved through supplementary demand in the Second (Monsoon) Session of the Legislature in July, 2023 to make funds available to the Corporation, are hereby made available to the Maharashtra State Divyang Finance and Development Corporation through this Government Resolution.

2. Before drawing the funds available on the Budget Distribution System (BEAMS) from the Treasury, the guiding instructions mentioned in paragraphs 4 to 18 and guidelines in the Appendix of the Finance Department's Circular dated 12.04.2023 should be strictly implemented.

4. For the said funds, Deputy Secretary (Divyang Welfare-2), Divyang Welfare Department, Mantralaya, Mumbai will be the Controlling Officer. The amount of the said grant is being handed over to the Regional Deputy Commissioner, Social Welfare, Navi Mumbai for further action. As per Finance Department G.R. No. Mumbidin-2022/C.R.24/2022/Viniyam, dt. 12.01.2023, the said grant is conditional. They are being given the authority to disburse the said grant to the Maharashtra State Divyang Finance and Development Corporation as per the bill format in M.K.N. 44. Immediately after the expenditure of the said funds, the detailed statement of expenditure and utilization certificate regarding the same should be submitted to the Government and the Accountant General's Office.
