The preamble is:

According to various study reports of the Central Board of Pollution Control, vehicle pollution is a major source of air pollution. Due to increased pollution levels, 18 cities in Maharashtra have been included in the polluted cities. These cities are mainly found to be above the pollution limit.

In this regard, according to the decision taken at the meeting held on 10/12/2020,

Maharashtra Electric Vehicle Policy for the Rapid Adoption of Vehicle Technology 2018, Government

Decree-Dispute 2017 / Procedure.188/Industry-2, dated 14/02/2018 to amend
