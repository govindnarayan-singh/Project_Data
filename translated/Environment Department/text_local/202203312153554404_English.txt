The State Lakes Conservation Scheme has been initiated by the State Government from the financial year 2006-07 under the State Scheme for Environmental Conservation of Lakes, Lakes and Large Reservoirs in the State. The scheme generally includes: 1. Determination of sources of pollution of the lake water by taking measures to prevent pollution. 2. Removal of essential hazardous and organic fertilizer contained in the lake. 3. To maintain the quality of water.
