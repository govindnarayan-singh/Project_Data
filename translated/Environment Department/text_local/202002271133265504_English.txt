Union Ministry of Environment, Forests and Environmental Change Solid Waste Management Regulations 2016

In the original application No. 606/2018, filed at M.N. Green Lodge on 24th January, 2020 in connection with the implementation of Solid Waste Management Rules 2016, the N.L.D. has directed the establishment of an Environment monitoring cell in the Office of the Chief Secretary.
