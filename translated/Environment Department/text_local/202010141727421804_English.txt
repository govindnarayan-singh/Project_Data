The preface:

To ensure a balanced commitment to nature, the Department of Environmental and Environmental Change is working on five aspects related to nature: Earth, Gas, Water, Fire, and Sky. To work on Earth-related forests, forestry, solid waste management, wastewater management and land reclamation, etc. To protect the air quality, we must reduce air pollution and improve air quality. Therefore, it was under consideration of the Government to implement the 'Majhi Vasundhara' campaign to bring together the local self-government bodies in the state to adopt a sustainable nature-friendly lifestyle by taking action based on these five principles related to nature.

Government decision:

Taking into account the provisions of the Resolution, the Government is approving the implementation of the 'Majhi Vasundhara' Mission based on the five principles related to the nature of earth, gas, water, fire and sky for the period of six months from 2 October 2020 to 31 March 2021 in the following 667 local self-government bodies in the State.
