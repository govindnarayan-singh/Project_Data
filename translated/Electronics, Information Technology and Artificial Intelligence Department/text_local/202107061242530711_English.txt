The preface:

Under Section 33 of the Right of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016, all administrative departments have been notified by the Government of India under the sub-notification dated 04 January 2021 to ensure that various categories of posts in the government service are secured for persons with disabilities. Under the sub-notification dated 2 February 2021 by the Department of Social Justice and Special Assistance, all administrative departments have been notified by the Government of India under the sub-notification dated 18 February 2021.

Government decision:

The date of the Committee of Experts constituted for the appointment of persons with disabilities in Government Service is 21 June.
