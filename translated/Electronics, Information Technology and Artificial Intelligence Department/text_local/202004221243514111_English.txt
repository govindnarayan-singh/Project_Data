The preamble is as follows:-

Governance Decision General Administration (Information Technology) Division No. Matanshan-1717/Pr.C.111/39 dated 09/04/2019 sets out the methodology for technical manpower supply by Maharashtra Information Technology Corporation. The appendix attached to the Sadar Jasan decision sets out the technical manpower rate (except manpower) for the period from 01/04/2019 to 31/03/2020.

Decisions of the Government:-

In accordance with the Government Decree No. 1 to 5, all Ministry departments, all Regional Offices under their jurisdiction, Public Enterprises, Corporations, Companies and Institutions established under an Act passed by the Central Government or State Government shall be subject to the Appendix to this Government Decree for the period from 01/04/2020 to 31/03/2021 for the acquisition of Technical Human Resources from the Maharashtra Information Technology Corporation for IT and IT Assistive Services. However, the Technical Human Resources of the Institutions Suppliing Technical Human Resources to Maharashtra Information Technology Corporation shall be available under the Appendix to this Government Decree for the period from 01/04/2020 to 31/03/2021.
