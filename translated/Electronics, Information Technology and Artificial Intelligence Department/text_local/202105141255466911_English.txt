The preamble is as follows:-

A Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) Action Committee has been set up at the State level by the relevant Governing Decision to regulate the action being taken through the various departments to provide the benefits of various welfare schemes implemented by the State Government/Central Government directly to the beneficiaries through Direct Benefit Transfer (8).

The Government was considering restructuring the Action Committee after the four-year period had expired and the old members were no longer functioning and new members were to be recruited.

Government charter -

The Direct Benefit Transfer Action Committee (DBT Cell) established at the State level by Government Decree, Department of General Administration, Matas 048/39 /2098/38, dated 16th September, 2016 is being restructured as follows.
