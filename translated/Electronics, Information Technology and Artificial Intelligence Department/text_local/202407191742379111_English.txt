The preamble is as follows:-

In order to avoid wasting the labour, money and time required to obtain various government services to the general public and to provide them with information technology at the prescribed time through the facilities of various departments at the same place in each district and taluka, the establishment of the facilities at each district and taluka was decided by Government Decree No. C.O.M.-1002/pr.kr.240/02/39, dated 23rd August, 2002 in order to avoid waste of labour, finance and time required to obtain various government services.

The comprehensive guidelines are being issued as follows due to the need to maintain the Setu Centres at the district headquarters and Tahsil offices in order not to cause inconvenience to the citizens.

Government [debt :- decision :-

1, All Setu Centres at the district headquarters and Tahsil offices should be maintained.

2. Districts in which the agencies of SETU Centres have expired or districts in which

The tendering process for opening of bridge centres in districts where bridge centres have been closed is urgent.

The relevant District Magistrate should initiate and complete the tender process in the next 3 months.

The district governor and Tahsil office should operate the Setu centers.

3. The District Setu Committee shall conduct a transparent tendering process to award Setu Centres to private contractors on a contractual basis. The tenderers shall conduct the tendering process according to the various instructions given by the Government. The terms and conditions of the tender shall be decided by the District Governor and shall include the instructions issued by the Government.

The initial term shall be three years. The new tendering process shall be conducted after the expiry of the contract. However, if the new tendering process is not implemented for some essential reason ST, the new tendering process may be extended to a maximum of two times each time in one year.

4. ad Contractual disputes during the term of the contract after contracting with the tenderers

The relevant Departmental Commissioner shall remain as the Arbitrator to resolve the issue if it arises.

5. The rates of facilities of the Setu Facilities Centre shall be based on the existing Government Decisions.

Rate will be charged and revenue will be distributed in a standard manner.

6. The decision on whether to subdivide all Setu Centres in the district to one or more contractors shall be taken by the District Governor of the respective district taking into account the local situation.

7. Government Orders issued from time to time regarding Setu Centres shall be binding on the Contractor.

The conditions of residence will have to be stipulated in the contract by the Governor.

8. In districts where the right contractor is not available/willing to operate the Centre on a contractual basis or one contractor has been dismissed for misconduct/irregularity and a second suitable contractor is not available, the district/taluka level bridge centres should be operated through the District Setu Committee, including the expenditure of the necessary resources, infrastructure, manpower etc. to operate the Centre through the District Setu Committee.

9. The Governor and the relevant Governor of the District shall have full responsibility for the management of the Setu Centres in the District Magistrate and Customs Office. The Governor shall ensure that certificates are distributed from the Setu Centres at government rates. Citizens may file complaints with the Governor if they have any complaints about misconduct/ irregularities in the Setu Centres.

10. Any complaints relating to the tendering process shall be submitted to the District Magistrate and Chairman, District Seetu Committee and the District Magistrate and Chairman, District Seetu Committee shall have the power to make the final decision in this process.

02. Administrative action against the Setu Centres: The District Magistrate (President District Setu Committee) and the relevant tasildar shall have full responsibility for the proper management of the Setu Centres in the District Magistrate and Tasil office. The District Magistrate and Tasildar shall ensure that services from the Setu Centres are provided at government rates. 1. If any complaint of malpractice/irregularity in the Setu Centres is received by the citizens, the complaint may be lodged with the relevant Sub-Divisionary Officer (Mahs). The relevant Sub-Divisionary Officer (Mohs) shall make a decision within 21 days on the complaint.
