The preamble -

The Department of Telecommunications guidelines dated 01.08.2013 have recommended the establishment of State-level and District-level Committees to deal with complaints arising from Telecommunications Infrastructure Construction.

Government decision:

District-level Telecommunications Committees are being constituted for each district under the Chairmanship of the Governor to address any difficulties in the construction of telecommunication towers and infrastructure as follows: -

a district attorney.

Two deputy forest rangers (DCF) members.

Three members of the police commissioner.

Four Deputy District Attorneys (RDC) organizers.

Five executives, five members of S.B.I.

6 Commissioner/CEO/CEO members

Seven members of the board of directors of every organization that builds telecom infrastructure.

8 Representatives from the Telecommunications Department LSA members

9 executive vice president, general counsel.

The functions of the District Level Telecommunications Committee will be as follows:

1. The District Level Telecommunications Committee will address pending permits for the construction of new/existing towers as well as pending permissions for the right of way of optical fiber cable (OFC ROW), taxation issues and complaints related to telecommunications infrastructure in the district.
