The preamble is as follows:-

All the administrative departments of the Government and their subordinate offices in the State are administered by the Maharashtra Information Technology Corporation (Maharashtra Information Technology Corporation (MIT) in the form of online examination through the Maharashtra Examination Portal in the context of Government Decree No. 1 of the Department of General Administration (MATANS). The Maharashtra Examination is conducted by Maharashtra Information Technology Corporation (MIT). The Maharashtra Examination is administered by Maharashtra from the same Service Provider (VENDER).

Decisions of the Government:-

This is a summary of the previous Governing Decisions of the Government dated September 19, 2017.

The following decisions have been authorized and taken by the Governments.

WSIS (Service Provider) has introduced a new tender procedure for conducting examination procedures in connection with the recruitment of Group A and Group D of various government departments.

The process of empanelment will be carried out through MahayIT.
